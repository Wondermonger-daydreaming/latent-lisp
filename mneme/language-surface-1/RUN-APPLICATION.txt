  THE RATIONALE, CORRECTED IN ERRATA 0.1.  Candidate /0 said a receipt for
  a non-deterministic expansion 'would be an account that could not be true
  twice.'  THAT WAS WRONG, and the error was worth catching: a receipt
  accounts for ONE OCCURRENCE, and a nondeterministic occurrence can have a
  perfectly truthful receipt — it says what form became what other form ON
  THAT OCCASION, which is what a receipt is for.

  THE REAL REASON IS REPRESENTABILITY.  The term grammar cannot account for
  uninterned-symbol identity INJECTIVELY: two distinct gensyms bearing one
  name collapse to a single identifier datum, because CD/0 compares
  identifier segments bytewise.  The grammar therefore cannot represent the
  binding structure that makes such a symbol mean anything.  That is a
  grammar limit, not an epistemic one, and it is the whole reason.

  SURFACE /0 IS STILL NOT MODIFIED TO MAKE THIS CONVENIENT.
