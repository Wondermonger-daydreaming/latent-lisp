DISPOSITION
==========================================================================
  NO production-API-resolving helper in any current executable instrument
  manufactures symbols.  All resolve through FIND-SYMBOL and refuse unless
  the symbol is :EXTERNAL.

  REMAINING INTERN CALLS ARE FIXTURE CONSTRUCTION, NOT HELPER RESOLUTION,
  and are correct as they stand.  They fall in three kinds:
    * probe-package fixtures  — (intern "X" pkg) in E03R-RHO / PROBE-P / …,
      symbols created precisely so the layer can be asked about them;
    * definition names inside source forms under test — (intern "*S1-N*"
      '#:cl-user), the name a specimen DEFINE-… form is about to bind;
    * a negative check that DEPENDS on interning — APPLICATION.lisp:186
      asserts the interned name is NOT bound.
  None of these resolves an operation of the production API.  The distinction
  is not stylistic: manufacturing a FIXTURE name is the instrument's subject;
  manufacturing an API name is the instrument losing the ability to fail.

  OUT OF SCOPE, REPORTED NOT REPAIRED: surface1.lisp and package.lisp are the
  production layer.  This addendum is not authorized to change them.  For the
  record, surface1.lisp resolves reconstructed symbols with FIND-SYMBOL and
  never INTERN by its own documented law (surface1.lisp:626), and validates
  status rather than mere accessibility (surface1.lisp:739).
