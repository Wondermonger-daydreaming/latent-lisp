## 2. THE CENTRAL TEST, APPLIED

*Can both inhabitants be expressed through one small public mechanism without
weakening either one's exactness, moving authority into the substrate, or
inventing abstractions unsupported by executable use?* — **No.** Three
independent failures:

**(a) Recognition cannot be shared, parameterized, or externalized without
loss.** de-pignore's relation is not a comparison over available facts — it is
an *invocation*: run the governed `derive/2` now, and re-anchor liveness
against the registry now. A public layer could carry it only by (i) importing
Slice /1 + Slice /2 into the layer — a Slice-shaped ministry, and authority
moved into the substrate's machinery for every other tenant; (ii) accepting a
caller-computed verdict — but then the layer's receipt certifies *testimony*
where de-pignore's today certifies that *the probe ran inside the transition*,
a strict weakening of the first tenant's exactness (its R1 liveness law —
"the same wrapper object cannot make a stale context look live", P:481 — is
exactly what dies when the recognition event leaves the transition); or (iii)
a declared-relation parameter rich enough to express "invoke this operation
and inspect that admission" — a rule language, i.e. an evaluator disguised as
an obligation checker, which the owner's charge and both specimens' own
headers forbid. de-vadimonio's relation, by deliberate contrast, consults
nothing — CENSOR made the difference executable: *mutate the world between
election and closing; de-pignore's answer changes, de-vadimonio's cannot.*
One relation is world-indexed at recognition time; the other is
retention-indexed at election time. **These are different offices, and the
location where discharge becomes recognized — the thing a lawful abstraction
must preserve — is in different buildings.**

**(b) The obligation record itself does not unify.** One tenant's record must
retain a live object reference (`base-anchor`, EQ against the registry); the
other's retains only immutable values and would be *wrong* to hold a live
anchor (its world-blindness is its law). A common record type needs a
one-tenant liveness field — a parameter with one user is an application field
wearing a kernel's name.

**(c) The owner's sketched kernel node "non-discharge receipt" has one
executable user.** The desertion species exists only in de-vadimonio — and
not as vocabulary: it is *forced* by retained-fact recognition (the attempt
happens outside the doors, so its failure must be carried back in as a
record). de-pignore can never need it (its probe is inside the door; failure
is a refusal at the door). An abstraction node supported by one inhabitant is
exactly what the charge excludes.

What survives the test — the **genuine common structure** — is real and is
named in §3. It is a *discipline*, not a mechanism.

## 3. THE FOUR NAMINGS OUTCOME B REQUIRES

**(1) The exact common structure that exists.** Both specimens are instances
of one executable discipline, inherited from Kernel /0's unresolved-effect
shape (`uncertain-effect.lisp:52`, `folds.lisp:421`) over non-effect
subjects: *election* (an explicit act + intent + scope; the world-fact never
opens an obligation by itself — `:no-elected-intent` refused in both, P:917,
V:`[010]`) · the *occurrence/content identity split* (distinct acts over same
content: distinct occurrence, equal content — both) · *fold-derived openness*
(no status slot, no registry, caller-supplied ordered history, receipts close
only on an exact conjunction, refusals never enter the fold — both) ·
*history truth* (immutable records; later success rewrites nothing — both) ·
*substrate-blindness* (the governed machinery never consults the obligation,
proven by control in both) · the shared ceilings (no persistence, no global
exactly-once — both, demonstrated not solved). This is a **law-set that
travels by precedent-copying with adversarial review**, and it has now
survived that transmission twice (de-pignore: 3 review rounds; de-vadimonio:
CENSOR + repair round with disease-reintroduction).

**(2) The exact irreducible difference preventing a public layer.** The
**recognition-authority boundary**: de-pignore recognizes by a *live
admissibility event* (invoke `derive/2` now; re-anchor liveness now; truth
indexed to the recognition-time world), de-vadimonio by a *retained identity
fact* (compare recorded facts against election-time retention; truth
deliberately blind to the recognition-time world). A shared layer must either
carry both offices (importing one tenant's substrate, or accepting testimony
in place of the other's in-transition proof) or parameterize recognition into
a rule language. Every route weakens an existing exactness or mints an
evaluator. Secondary irreducibles, both consequences of the primary: the
live-anchor field, and the desertion species.

**(3) Why ordinary helper reuse is sufficient.** The shape has already been
reused twice *as law*, not as linked code — each tenant re-instantiates it
over its own types, with its own conjuncts, under review that checked the
laws bit. Nothing was lost in either transmission that a shared library would
have preserved; what a shared library would *add* is a public surface coupling
two immutable application-private specimens to a third artifact none of their
evidence exercises, plus the false affordance that instantiating the library
confers the laws (it would confer the fold, not the teeth — and the teeth are
where both specimens' defects actually lived). Discipline-reuse is not the
absence of reuse; it is the form of reuse whose failure mode — drifting from
the law — is exactly what the review culture demonstrably catches.

**(4) What third inhabitant or new law would warrant reopening.** Any of:
- **a third tenant whose recognition is retained-fact** (world-blind
  comparison over already-produced objects, de-vadimonio's office): the
  *retained-recognition* office would then hold two executable users, and a
  mechanism for that office alone — narrower than "Obligation /0" — becomes
  askable on evidence;
- **a substrate-provided liveness fact**: if Slice /1 ever exports a
  registry-epoch or anchor-identity such that "this schema is the currently
  registered one" becomes a comparable *value* rather than an in-image EQ
  event, de-pignore's live office could be re-expressed as retained-fact
  recognition, and the two offices merge — that is a new substrate law and an
  owner decision, not an obligation-layer patch;
- **the Mneme journal store (the board's arc 3)**: both tenants' deepest
  shared ceiling is the caller-supplied history (no persistence, no global
  exactly-once, both stated in both files). A governed journal changes what a
  fold *is*; the obligation question would need re-asking on that ground
  regardless of the count of tenants.
