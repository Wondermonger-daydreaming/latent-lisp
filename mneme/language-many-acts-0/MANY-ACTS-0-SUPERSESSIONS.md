## 0. THE REGISTRY'S OWN LAW — READ BEFORE ADDING A ROW

1. **APPEND-ONLY. A correction to an existing entry is made by a NEW CORRECTING ENTRY, never
   by silently rewriting the old row.** An entry that is wrong stays on the page with its
   number, and the correcting entry names it. Nothing here is ever deleted or edited in
   place; the only lawful change to this file is an addition at the end.
2. **Returned reports and frozen observations remain byte-untouched.** This registry exists
   *because* they may not be annotated. Filing a row is not permission to touch the artifact
   the row names.
3. **A superseded statement was not false when it was written.** Every entry below records a
   figure that was a true observation at its own moment and is superseded for prospective
   use. Recording it here is not an accusation and does not diminish the original record.
4. **Filing is part of the superseding commit**, not a follow-up. A round that supersedes a
   figure files its row in the same commit that supersedes it.
5. **Each entry names, at minimum:** the exact historical artifact and locus (file plus
   section/line), the statement as written (quoted), the governing instrument, and the
   current governing statement.

Line numbers cited below are as of the artifact's byte-state at commit `48e59db3`, the base
