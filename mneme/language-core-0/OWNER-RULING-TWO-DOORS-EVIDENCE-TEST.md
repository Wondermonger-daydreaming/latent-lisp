evidence) and **`perform`** (effect-capable consequence, new) — over **one

## Verdict of the test

**The instinct survives the census. No concrete contradiction found.** Three
findings — one refinement the evidence forces, one deep confirmation, one
asymmetry that must be preserved — and a graded inventory of what "shared
substrate" may honestly mean today.

### Finding 1 — REFINEMENT FORCED: "possibility" must mean *language-accounted* possibility

The raw criterion ("may cross an external effect frontier") is collapsed by the
frozen evidence in one place: **`exercise-value`** (Slice /0, closed) invokes a
host closure under authorization — and a host closure *can* do anything to the
world. More broadly, the quiet zone is Common Lisp: any stratum-2 form can
launch missiles. Under the raw criterion, everything is effect-capable and the
distinction dies.

The frozen evidence already contains the repair: Slice /0's escape inventory
and strata doctrine distinguish what the language **accounts for** from what
the host can do. So the criterion holds as: **`perform` is the sole form under
which an external effect is *lawful and accounted* — prepared, authorized,
journaled, manifested, outcome-ized. External effects produced anywhere else
are unaccounted host actions, already receipted as stratum-2/3 honesty, not
performances.** `exercise-value` stays exactly what its closure says it is: a
governed *invocation* whose canonical-result gate refuses laundering, silent
about what the closure did to the world — an acknowledged escape (annex 1 §4,
E-family), not a rival door.

### Finding 2 — DEEP CONFIRMATION: the criterion reproduces the frozen machinery's own boundary

The strongest evidence *for* the frontier criterion is that the closed bytes
already carve at that exact joint:

- **`check-retry-safety` / the no-blind-retry law exists only on the effectful
  side** — blind retry is dangerous precisely and only when an external
  frontier may have been crossed without witnessed settlement (kernel §14.1,
  the fold's UNC discipline).
- **`derive` is always safely re-runnable**: deterministic over supplied
  supports, fresh `:receipt` identity per attempt (census annex 2, row 48 —
  "distinct across re-derivations"), and there is no external world for a
  re-derivation to double-strike. The frozen design gives derive *no*
  interruption standing, *no* retry machinery, *no* indeterminate decision —
  and under the owner's criterion that is not a gap; it is the criterion's
  prediction, already in the bytes.

The retry/reconciliation/indeterminacy obligations attach exactly where the
ruling says they must. The evidence and the instinct carve at the same joint.

### Finding 3 — ASYMMETRY THAT MUST BE PRESERVED: decision arity

`derive`'s decision is **binary** — granted/refused — by the owner's own
multiplicity ruling ("Decision stays binary granted/refused", CHARTER-DELTA-2),
with the six premise dispositions carried structurally beneath it. `perform`'s
outcome view is **irreducibly three-way** — :refused / :committed /
:indeterminate — because the world can withhold the answer; :indeterminate is
the entire epistemic point of the effectful frontier. Therefore "common
outcome-handling forms" is supported by the evidence **at the shared-grammar
level only**: the `why` door (one uniform inspection, extractor registry — both
doors registrable), typed refusal conditions carrying their receipts, and
whitelisted repair restarts. A single unified outcome-projection spanning both
doors would either flatten derive's structure or mint an indeterminate
evidentiary decision that closed law forbids. **No unified projection.**
