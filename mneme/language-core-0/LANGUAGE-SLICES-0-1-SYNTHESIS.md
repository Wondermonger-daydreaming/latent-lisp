## §0. The finding that shapes everything else: TWO frontiers, one discipline

The charge speaks of "consequential computation" as one category. The frozen
evidence contains **two distinct consequential frontiers**, at different stages of
existence:

1. **The evidentiary frontier** — the acts by which a program says something
   *stronger than it has said*: `raise` (Slice /0) and `derive` (Slice /1), plus
   the travel acts `project-claim` / `transmit` / `exercise-value`. This frontier
   **exists, is closed, and runs today** (66/0 + 50/0 + 12/12 + 14/14 + 9/9 across
   the shipped batteries). Its discipline: typed refusals naming the missing
   relation, receipts on every path, lawful repairs, no scalar standing anywhere.

2. **The effectful frontier** — the acts by which a program *changes something
   outside itself*: attempts, authority, adapters, manifestations, journal,
   interruption. This frontier is **spec-governed but surface-less**. The kernel0
   implementation is a pure data+fold+validation core: **371 exports, all nouns;
   zero of Kernel §19's operational verbs are implemented** (census annex 3 §0,
   chair-re-verified by grep). Of the ten lowering steps, exactly one (outcome
   construction) is fully live as an operation; four more have their complete
   record/fold layer live with no verb; five have no runtime at all.

**The synthesis's central adjudication:** these are two wings of ONE discipline —
in both, the language refuses to let a result outrun its evidence — and the
smallest executable center treats them asymmetrically, because the evidence does:
the evidentiary wing is *extracted* (it exists), the effectful wing is *bridged*
(the smallest surface-to-kernel seam is proposed, graded, and gated). Anything
that presents the effectful wing as already-existing language would be a claim
wearing a costume.

**On "one clear front door" (adjudicated, with grounds):** the charge asks for one
front door for consequential computation. The frozen evidence forecloses a single
umbrella form: `derive` is closed law and cannot be re-wrapped without reopening
Slice /1 (a non-goal), and the slices' own precedent is a **family of governed
acts with one shared grammar** — `(values result receipt)`, typed refusal
conditions carrying receipts, lawful-repair restarts, `why` as the one uniform
explanation door. The adjudication: **"one front door" is satisfied at the grammar
level, not the form level.** The effectful wing gets exactly ONE new governed act
(§3), family-resemblant to `raise`/`derive`; no umbrella macro re-surfaces the
closed acts. A future unifying form remains available as successor pressure,
unopened. (The chair flags this ruling for owner ratification in §5 — it answers a
question the chair itself raised, and the owner may overrule.)

## §2. The smallest executable core — what Lisp+ already is

### 2a. The quiet zone (already licensed, stratum 2)

Ordinary Lisp+ **is Common Lisp**. The four-strata doctrine (annex 1 §2, quoted
from the frozen architecture) makes stratum 2 — "everything CL lawfully
provides" — part of the language's own model. The shipped programs prove it in
the bytes: every specimen freely uses plain CL for helpers, data, control; no
constructor, act, or receipt attaches to any of it. **No standing exists in the
quiet zone at all** — the charter's separated-categories model means an ordinary
value is not "unverified"; it is simply not a claim. Ordinary conditions and
restarts operate in the CL tradition, wholly distinct from governed refusals
(the `with-slice0-restarts` whitelist governs *governed* signalling sites only).

This kills the failure-shape the charge names (*"an evidence protocol with
parentheses"*) by construction: consequence begins only where a program
**constructs a claim or invokes a governed act** — and both are explicit,
single-colon, public forms.

### 2b. The evidentiary center (already executable; extraction, not design)

The smallest set of forms that demonstrates Lisp+ is more than its two
reductions:

| Wing | Forms |
|---|---|
| entry into governance | `claim` · `witness` · `proposition` · `proposition-pattern` · `refutation` · `local-value` |
| schema/procedure anatomy | `promotion-procedure` · `judgment-schema` · `register-schema` · `resolve-schema` |
| position | `receiver-context` · `support-store` |
| **the governed acts** | **`raise` · `derive`** (say-something-stronger) · **`project-claim` · `transmit` · `exercise-value`** (travel) · `transported-testimony` (evidence-that) |
| explanation | `why` (one uniform door) · `render-why` · `render-derivation-why` · the views |
| refusal + repair | the 13 + 8 typed conditions · the 12 lawful restarts |

**Against reduction 1 ("Common Lisp plus a library"):** the honest form of this
claim is the closure's own, quoted at its size: the fragment is *"constructively
library-reproducible (each substrate file is portable CL)"* — Slice /0 never
claimed irreducibility, and this synthesis does not either. What the evidence
does license: the acceptance test **"can a disciplined library reproduce this"**
was answered by demonstration — the ablations ARE that disciplined library, and
each one reproduced the misleading move its slice exists to refuse (the ladder
laundered, the status copied, the boolean flattened, the S3 species — twice, in
two domains). What distinguishes Lisp+ from the library is not implementability
but **what the surface makes unsayable-or-conspicuous**: a judgment without a
procedure, testimony flattened to direct support, a conclusion granted with
undeclared anatomy, plurality silently collapsed. That is a *language* property —
it lives in what programs can be written, not in how the substrate is built.

**Against reduction 2 ("Mneme exposed directly as a programming interface"):**
decisive and mechanical — **the slices re-export zero kernel0 symbols** (annex 1
§0; annex 2 §1B). 161 + 69 exported symbols, none of them kernel nouns. Kernel /0
is substrate the acts *drive* (`derive` → frozen `raise` → kernel0 procedure
gate); the programmer never touches a kernel record to compute evidentially. The
language surface and the memory/continuity substrate are already separated in the
bytes — which is the charge's governing standing, discovered rather than imposed.

### 2c. What the center still lacks (the honest gap)

The center has **no effectful front door**. Nothing in 230 exported symbols can
change anything outside the image and return a structured account of what
happened. Kernel /0 §19.8 names the pipeline; no surface reaches it. That gap —
and only that gap — is what Language Core /0 (§4, work order) is licensed to
bridge.

## §3. The surface-to-kernel lowering contract (candidate, graded per clause)

The contract for the ONE new effectful governed act. Working name **`perform`**
(public name earned by specimen, per the §12-precedent of both slices; `attempt`
is foreclosed — it is a kernel record noun). Shape follows the slices' grammar,
not new invention:

```lisp
;; CANDIDATE SURFACE — illustrative, non-normative
(perform :adapter    <adapter-designator>     ; which door to the outside
         :request    <canonical-request>      ; CD/0-lawful data, never a host object
         :authority  <capability>             ; explicit, never ambient
         :process    <process-context>)       ; the consequence context (see below)
;; => (values outcome evidence)               ; outcome = kernel0 4-axis record
;; pre-frontier refusals SIGNAL a typed condition carrying the outcome-so-far
```

`process-context` is a **constructor-made object** (`consequence-context`,
following the `receiver-context` precedent), not a `with-…` macro. Sol's
`with-consequence` macro shape is recorded as an **experimental alternative**:
the slices' precedent is constructors + explicit arguments; a binding macro adds
surface without adding semantics, and macro-established ambient authority is
exactly what "explicit, never ambient" forbids. If ergonomic pressure from the
specimens demands a macro, it must be a thin wrapper that still passes the
capability explicitly (grade at that time: implementation-forced or ergonomic,
with the receipt).

### 3b. The outcome projection — defining what Sol's three-way match lawfully means

`match-outcome` is named in Kernel §19.6 but unimplemented, and the outcome
record is a **4-axis × per-axis-determinacy lattice** (DK-4), not a three-state
tag. The three-way surface view is therefore a **projection**, and the contract
defines it rather than letting convenience erase the lattice:

```text
outcome-kind (a VIEW function, precedent: derivation-receipt-multiply-supported-p):
  :refused        iff the attempt terminated pre-frontier — no frontier-crossed
                  event exists in the sequence (Kernel §12.6); the typed refusal
                  names the failed precondition
  :committed      iff fold-attempt-outcome yields a settled terminal class with
                  effect-axis determinacy :determinate and no unresolved
                  uncertain-effect standing
  :indeterminate  otherwise (unresolved uncertain-effect, bounded/indeterminate
                  effect axis, or non-terminal)
```

Grade: **conservative authorial completion**, anchored in §12.6 + §13.6 + the
implemented fold; the exact boolean composition is validated against spec text at
implementation time, and **divergences adjudicate to spec text** (PJ0 precedent).
Three laws ride with it: (1) the full 4-axis record is always returned — the view
never replaces it; (2) no boolean summary exists anywhere (`success-p` is
unmintable, the slices' no-boolean law extended); (3) a `match-outcome` surface
macro binding by view-class is **deferred library convenience** — recorded, not
built (the syntax-proliferation guard).

What `perform` must never return: the bare manifestation value. Sol's charge
put it exactly; the contract adopts it as law: *a bare value has amputated the
execution context — it cannot say whether the effect was prepared, refused,
committed, duplicated, simulated, or reconstructed.*

(`with-outcome`, named in §19.6 with no specified semantics anywhere: **refused
outright** — pure invention would be required; it is returned to the successor
spec round as an open item, not smuggled in as an implementation choice.)

### 4b. THE BRASS COURIER — one effectful consequence through the fake adapter

**Purpose:** the first crossing of the effectful frontier. A small surface call
lowers into the full §19.8 pipeline and returns a structured outcome.

**Source (illustrative):**
```lisp
(let* ((ctx (consequence-context :authority courier-key))   ; capability explicit
       (multiple-value-bind (outcome evidence)
           (perform :adapter 'fake-courier
                    :request (proposition '(:predicate :deliver
                                            (:payload "The orchard remembers.")))
                    :authority courier-key :process ctx)
         (ecase (outcome-kind outcome)
           (:committed     (list :delivered (manifestation-of outcome)))
           (:refused       (list :not-sent  (refusal-of outcome)))
           (:indeterminate (list :unknown   (standing-of outcome)))))))
```

**The fake adapter's world:** a deterministic in-memory courier **ledger** inside
the adapter — adopted from Sol's design because it enacts the distinction that is,
in Sol's words, pure Lisp+ marrow: *the letter being placed in the ledger is not
identical to the program recording that it placed the letter there.* The effect
(ledger row) and the testimony (manifestation record + events) are separate
records the battery compares.

**Expected observable result:** a 4-axis outcome whose view is `:committed`, whose
manifestation carries the receipt token (e.g. `"fake:courier:0001"`) — never the
bare payload string.
**Expected process evidence:** the in-memory event sequence showing, in lawful
order: attempt minted → authority checked → adapter selected → effect prepared →
invocation → manifestation recorded → outcome constructed. `validate-event-
sequence` passes over it; **and the refusal arm is exercised too** (wrong-scope
capability ⇒ typed pre-frontier refusal, no frontier event, no ledger row).
**Forbidden shortcuts:** returning the bare value; an ack acquiring settling
force (AP-ACK-4); authority read from ambient state; a boolean success flag.
**Settles:** the front-door syntax; how authority enters (explicit argument);
adapter naming; the outcome projection's exact behavior; how much kernel
vocabulary surfaces (records reach the surface; verbs do not).
**Leaves open (deliberately):** durability (nothing survives image death, said
plainly); streams; cancellation; live authority breadth; the ergonomic-macro
question.
