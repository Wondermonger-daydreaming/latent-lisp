# Post-Reveal Retrospective

## What I inferred correctly

The central inference was correct: scholarly quotation admission had to be a
structured conclusion whose receiver and purpose were conclusion variables, with
the edition-path anatomy represented by shared schema locals. In particular,
edition, passage, locator, source identifier, transcription check, provenance,
translation, and inspectable derivative needed coherent bindings across distinct
premises. That is precisely the Architecture's pattern-against-ground,
first-binding-then-consistency model.

I also inferred the multiplicity law correctly. Two complete edition paths are
plural support and must grant without traversal-order selection. Ambiguity begins
only when `:translation` is declared unique and two values survive across
complete environments. No meaning was inferred from names such as
`"edition-alpha"` or `"edition-beta"`.

Receiver and purpose relativity were represented in proposition anatomy rather
than special-cased. The reveal confirms that this ordinary binding coherence is
intentional. Refutation, inaccessibility residue, and transported testimony were
also represented at their intended levels.

## What I misunderstood

I initially treated the projection-side public-form derivation as if an ordinary
direct review witness could fill the `:derivation` argument. The API rejected that
flattening in behavior: redaction requires a `:derivation`-mode witness. I repaired
the mode before freeze.

The deeper point appeared only in Architecture: even though `witness` is exported,
a same-image hand-built derivation witness is the acknowledged D-forge,
stratum-3 host escape. My projection case demonstrates that `project-claim`
reconstructs rather than copies a source judgment, but its bridge witness is not
equivalent to invoking `derive` over Bob's seven premises. Therefore the initial
report's `:front-door-only t` is true in the narrow static sense (all referenced
symbols are exported) but needs this host-boundary rider. It must not be read as
host-level closure or as proof that the projection bridge itself was minted by
the governed derivation act.

## Did the reveal correct code or deepen explanation?

It deepened and bounded the explanation; it did not change the frozen program.
The executable result was already passing before reveal. The D-forge
classification changes how strongly the projection case may be described, not
the observed fact that the target proposition and judgment were reconstructed
instead of copied.

## Plurality and uniqueness

They were used as intended. The ordinary schema retains two complete binding
environments and grants with `multiply-supported-p` true. The designated-
translation schema retains those environments but refuses because and only
because `:translation` is in `:unique-locals`. The conflict names that local.

## Did I hide a premise?

No task-required premise is hidden inside the conclusion or a generic evidence
token. The seven required distinctions are seven premise predicates. The
projection bridge is a separate limitation: its hand-built derivation witness
does not add or erase a premise in the quotation-admission schema, but it is
outside the fully governed `derive` path and is therefore reported as such.

## Successor pressure from implementation friction

Before reveal, actual implementation friction suggested a public bridge from a
registered schema or derivation receipt to the projection procedure/witness
needed for lawful public-form reconstruction. The current surface exposes the
schema's admit-kind but not a governed operation that performs target projection
by re-running the schema over target-local premises. This pressure arose from the
task, not from Architecture or Closure.

The Architecture confirms the relevant integration seam but also shows why the
request must be carefully sized: closing D-forge would wrongly claim host-level
closure. A successor should add a governed convenience path, not pretend the host
escape can be abolished.

## Closure-contaminated suggestion

The Closure ranks a public Slice /0 `why`-extractor registration point as the
cleanest debt. I did not encounter that seam while implementing on exports and
would not have proposed it from this task. It is therefore contaminated by the
reveal and is not evidence from this stranger implementation.

Likewise, the Closure's suggestions about schema identity domains and wider
structured-proposition adoption did not arise from my implementation friction.
