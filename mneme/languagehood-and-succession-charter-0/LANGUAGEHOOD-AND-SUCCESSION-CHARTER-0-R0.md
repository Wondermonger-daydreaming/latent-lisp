**A.0 This is a candidate.** R0 proposes holdings; it adopts none of them. Nothing
in this charter becomes law by being written, reviewed, agreed with, cited,
committed, pushed, or directed by the commission that ordered it drafted
(Charter-P1 §0.3 and §11.6 state the same law for their own text; R0 binds itself
identically). A passing verifier, a clean load, a complete parcel, or a green
checksum file ratifies nothing (commission §1.7: *executable green is not
adoption*).

**A.1 The status legend.** Every classified proposition in this charter carries
exactly one of six statuses:

| Status | Meaning | Boundary clause |
|---|---|---|
| **ADOPTED LAW** | Owner-ratified and governing now. | Attaches only where an exact ruling or adoption instrument is cited. Descent from an adopted commit confers nothing (`MANY-ACTS-0-STANDING.md`: standing attaches to immutable identities and explicit dispositions, never to filenames or descent). |
| **ACCEPTED EVIDENCE** | An empirical or documentary result accepted under its recorded ceiling. | **Terminal.** An ACCEPTED EVIDENCE record is a terminus, not a staging area (Charter-P1 §2.4). **A.1a Scope note — propositions, not sources.** The six statuses classify

## §B — Jurisdictional thesis

**B.0 The central proposition.**

> **The substrate executes the judge; it is not itself the law.**

**Standing of this sentence: PROPOSED HOLDING** `[PROPOSED]`. It is the owner's
formulation, given in the commission of 2026-08-11; it has not been owner-ratified
as adopted law, and this charter does not quote it as though it were. Its
evidential kin, already at their own standings: the L0-a licensed sentence
(Charter-P1 §1.L0.3, resting on adopted One Act /0 and Surface Account /0
evidence) and the wire-format law *"JSON is plumbing, not semantic jurisdiction"*
(`NORMATIVE-OBSERVATION-FORMAT-0-CANDIDATE-P1.md`, candidate). Ratifying B.0 is
fork F-3.

**B.1 Six objects that must never be conflated.** Every languagehood argument in
this lane passes through six distinct objects. A claim true of one is not thereby
true of any other:

1. **The host reader/compiler/runtime** — SBCL executing Common Lisp. A fact
   about what the host *can run* is a fact about the host.
2. **The implementation of the Lisp+ judge/evaluator** — the `ma0-*.lisp` bodies
   and their kin: Common Lisp programs, candidate bytes at the MA0 layer, adopted
   bytes where an exact adoption says so.
3. **The Lisp+ normative specification** — the layer that *owns* meaning:
   adopted instruments (Architecture 0.1, Kernel /0 spec, PJ0, AP0, One Act /0,
   Surface Account /0) and candidate instruments (the MA0 grammar/contract/guide).
   Where the specification is silent, underdetermined, or unpublished, meaning is
   not silently inherited from object 2 — it is a **spec defect** (Charter-P1 §7;
   the 28-place register is the standing inventory of exactly this).
4. **A Lisp+ source form or program** — data under judgment.
5. **An observed execution trace or outcome** — what one run of objects 1+2
   produced from object 4, on one date, under one custody.
6. **An evidential claim about that outcome** — a sentence, carrying authorship,
   exposure, and standing qualifiers, which is the *only* object this charter
   classifies.

**B.2 What the thesis asserts.** Implementation in Common Lisp does not, by
itself, collapse Lisp+ semantics into ordinary Common Lisp evaluation — because
the *normative* answer to "what does this Lisp+ form mean?" is owed by object 3
and adjudicated through object 2's specified refusal-and-judgment behavior, not
by handing the form to `cl:eval`. The witnesses that this is a real jurisdictional
boundary and not a documentary pose are behavioral and already recorded at their
own standings: specification-owned refusals typed at the program level (V-BIND,
V-TERM, V-SHAPE, V-ATOMS — adopted as claim-ceiling law in R1 Riders 3–5),
refusal as a semantic *result* rather than a host error, the untaken arm as an
inspectable object, and disease witnesses that must stay red (Charter-P1 §8).

**B.3 What the thesis does not assert.** Four inversions, each refused:

- **REFUSED:** *"Common Lisp cannot express Lisp+ semantics."* Category error
  (Charter-P1 §1.L0.4 forbids it; the chair's reply to Sol concedes it). Host
  expressibility is the *ground* of the whole arrangement, not a rival claim.
- **REFUSED:** *"Common Lisp evaluation is the law of Lisp+ forms."* This is the
  inverse collapse. It is contradicted at adopted standing by the specification
  instruments that own judgment, and it would erase the refusal semantics those
  instruments specify.
- **REFUSED:** *"A specification document alone proves a functioning separate
  language."* Syntactic resemblance plus prose is not languagehood; the lattice's
  L0 properties are *witnessed*, not merely declared (§E rung 1).
- **REFUSED:** *"The jurisdictional boundary, once stated, is self-maintaining."*
  It is maintained by machinery — evaluator-modification prohibitions (Charter-P1
  §9), disease conservation (§8), freeze discipline (§3) — and lapses where the
  machinery lapses.

**B.4 Scale annotation, mandatory.** The thesis holds at two strata with two
standings (Ruling 8's stratification, carried at §E rung 1): at the
**adopted-seam scale** (One Act /0, Surface Account /0) the jurisdictional
boundary rests on ADOPTED LAW-witnessed evidence; at the **program-language
scale** (Many Acts /0 + R1) it rests on candidate bytes and contracts if the
candidate declines (Charter-P1's contraction edge). Any statement of B.0 that
does not name its stratum is understated as to one and overstated as to the
other.

**B.5 The enforcement perimeter is not the jurisdiction — and the lab already
banked this.** Semantic jurisdiction (C.2) is a *normative* property; it is not
host-enforced, and the lab's own ablation evidence says so at slice scope: the
Slice /0 de-promotione banked disposition
(`language-slice-0/de-promotione/DISPOSITION.md`, 2026-07-23, append-only)
separates `:semantic-model :validated` · `:public-surface
:embedded-language-kernel` · `:governed-language-act :earned` ·
`:host-level-enforcement :not-earned` · `:standalone-language-claim
:not-yet-earned`, records the escape surface (`:common-lisp-package-internals`
— one `::` suffices), and rules the reading this charter adopts as its own
`[PROPOSED]` at language scale: *"Host bypass is not evidence that the semantic
act does not exist; it is evidence about where the act's enforcement perimeter
currently sits."* Therefore B.0 must never be quoted as a host-closure or
tamper-resistance claim: the law governs what counts as a lawful Lisp+ outcome
through the governed surface; a host-level actor can bypass the surface, and
that bypass is a recorded perimeter finding, not a counter-example to
specification-ownership — and not a licensed excuse for it either. (This
clause is Sol-independent lab-side prior art for the thesis, thirteen days
older than the outside reading; evidence rows EV-45/EV-46.)

## §C — Defined vocabulary

*Each definition carries exclusion clauses. The definitions bind the use of these
terms **in this charter and in documents that cite it**; they do not retroactively
re-word any sealed instrument (§H). Where a term is P1-forbidden as a result
description, the definition says so on its face.*

**C.1 host expressibility.** The capacity of the host (object B.1.1) to read,
compile, host, and execute the machinery by which Lisp+ forms are judged.
*Excludes:* any normative claim. Host expressibility is a fact about the
substrate relationship; it neither grants Common Lisp jurisdiction over Lisp+
meaning nor subtracts from it.

**C.2 semantic jurisdiction.** The locus that *owns* the normative answer to
"what does this form mean / what must happen?" For Lisp+ forms, the proposed
locus is the Lisp+ specification layer, adjudicated through the specified judge
behavior. *Excludes:* possession by execution — running a form is not owning its
meaning; and completeness — jurisdiction over the specified cases says nothing
about cases the specification has not reached (the 28-place register bounds
exactly this).

**C.3 normative syntax; well-formedness.** The specification-owned grammar of
acceptable Lisp+ forms; well-formedness is conformity to it as judged by the
specified validator, including its refusals. *Excludes:* host readability — a
form the CL reader accepts is not thereby well-formed Lisp+; and the converse.

**C.4 operational semantics.** The specification-owned account of what judgment
and execution do to a well-formed form: evaluation, environment and binding
discipline, branch selection, terminal discipline, refusal typing, result
construction. *Excludes:* whatever the implementation happens to do in
unspecified territory (that is behavior, not semantics, until specified).

**C.5 observable consequence.** An outcome at the specified observable boundary
(judgments, refusals with their types, summaries, transcripts in the normative
observation format). *Excludes:* implementation-internal events; timings; host
resource behavior — unless the specification names them observable.

**C.6 languagehood.** The bounded property that a notation possesses
specification-owned normative syntax, validation, operational semantics, refusal
behavior, and observable consequences — i.e., objects B.1.3–B.1.5 exist and are
witnessed doing their jobs. *Excludes:* portability, implementation independence,
inhabitation, generality, fitness for any author population, and succession —
every one a separate node (§E). Languagehood is the floor of the lattice, not a
summary of it.

**C.7 language distinctness.** The claim that Lisp+ semantic jurisdiction is
distinct from ordinary Common Lisp evaluation (B.2). *Excludes:* the refused
inversions of B.3; any claim of host inability.

**C.8 embedded language / hosted language.** A language whose judge runs inside
a host runtime and whose surface may share the host's reader, while semantic
jurisdiction remains specification-owned (B.0). *Exclusion, load-bearing
(chair-corrected 2026-08-11 after re-search):* no lab instrument classifies
**Lisp+ as a whole** as a hosted/embedded language at adopted standing — for
the whole language the classification originates in an outside reading under
shared input (Sol, 2026-08-10) and in the owner's commission. The *frame* has
lab-side precedent at slice scope: the Slice /0 guide ("a small embedded
language fragment") and the de-promotione banked disposition
(`:public-surface :embedded-language-kernel`, with the standalone-language
claim recorded **not-yet-earned** and the enforcement perimeter's escape
surface named — §B.5). The term is defined here so that fork F-3 can ratify or
decline a precise sentence, not a vibe — and so that "embedded" never smuggles
a host-closure claim (§B.5 bars that reading).

**C.9 program expressibility.** The demonstrated capacity of a *specific* frozen
surface to express a *specific* program or specimen class, under stated
authorship and exposure conditions. *Excludes:* generality across programs,
authors, or domains. The only ACCEPTED EVIDENCE instance is R-P4 at exactly
Rider 1's sentence.

**C.10 semantic prediction.** An author's advance statement of a program's
observable consequences, later compared against the actual trace. *Excludes:*
any claim about *why* prediction succeeded (channel claims are C.11's business).

**C.11 semantic transmission.** A *channel* claim: that named public materials
alone carried the semantics from specification to author. *Exclusion,
load-bearing:* transmission is not agreement. P5's accepted record is
**semantic-agreement** evidence whose author had disclosed prior source and
transcript exposure; the owner's governing correction states the public guide
cannot be isolated as the sole transmission channel, and Charter-P1 §1.E.P5.4
refuses "guide-only semantic transmission" for it. **No transmission claim is
currently licensed for any Lisp+ artifact.**

**C.12 conformance; scope of a conformance claim.** Agreement of an
implementation's observable consequences with the canonical judge across a
**named frozen vector set**, reported first-run and repaired separately.
*Excludes:* semantic completeness — conformance to a finite set is conformance
to that set and the behaviors it covers, never proof that every normative case
is implemented correctly; and any unnamed-set generality. A conformance sentence
without its vector-set name and freeze identity is malformed.

**C.13 implementation independence.** A provenance property of a second
evaluator: independently designed and coded, without deriving from, consulting,
or transliterating the reference implementation (Charter-P1 Art. 4). *Excludes:*
substrate difference (C.15 exclusion); a rewrite by a construction-loop
participant, which is a PORT; and any inference from languagehood — a language
with one implementation has exactly one implementation.

**C.14 independently seeded implementation.** An implementation begun from the
frozen public normative packet only, behind an exposure fence, by hands outside
the construction orbit — the strong form of C.13, "independently SEEDED under
shared normative infrastructure" (the LCI/0 formulation), never shortened past
its seed conditions. *Excludes:* clean-room status after any reference-source
consultation (that consultation is disclosed, and the campaign re-classes as
interoperability work).

**C.15 substrate independence.** The travel of the semantics to a genuinely
different implementation substrate (different language/runtime, not merely a
second CL image). *Exclusion, on its face (P1 §4.3, §1.E.P5.4, both verbatim
elsewhere in this parcel):* **substrate difference is not independence, and
substrate difference is not orbit difference.** In the §D matrix this axis is
recorded and **contributes nothing to any other axis.**

**C.16 author independence.** The program author stands outside the
language-construction loop and relevant prior solutions, with exposure
disclosed and fenced. *Excludes:* "held out" — a held-out program by a
construction author is same-author evidence with a holdout condition (Rider 1's
exact shape), not author independence; and substrate difference (C.15).

**C.17 audit independence.** The reviewer/validator lacks the shared
construction roots that would compromise the asserted audit; the lab's two-tier
law applies (fresh-context same-model review is not fresh-weights review; a
sibling or model that read the conclusions is shared-input). *Excludes:*
"different model name" as a token; any audit by this chair or its agents —
including this charter — which is same-root by construction.

**C.18 model-family independence.** Authors/reviewers drawn from training
lineages separated enough for the claim being made (a claim-relative judgment,
declared per campaign). *Excludes:* provider or product-name difference as
proof; substrate difference (C.15). This axis exists in no predecessor
instrument and is defined here for the first time; it must never be scored by
brand name.

**C.19 independent inhabitation.** *P1-forbidden as a result description*
(Charter-P1 §1.CI.4). Defined here only as the **question** the CI0 node answers
under its owner-directed name **Clean-room outsider inhabitation /0**: a mind
outside the construction and adjudication orbit authors a program from public
materials that is accepted and behaves as its author intended, without evaluator
modification. *Excludes:* every existing artifact — the CI0 licensed sentence
has no instance; P5 is not one (owner ruling); one instance, if earned, is an
existence proof over one author, not a general result.

**C.20 generic authoring.** Authoring success across declared, materially
different tasks/topologies by authors of any provenance through the declared
public extension mechanism, without evaluator modification and without any
inherited disease witness going quiet — the DG0 question, author-agnostic by
Ruling 8. *Excludes:* "generic" as a result adjective for any finite run (finite
runs license only *"evidence of disease-conserving generativity across the
tested unseen application topologies"* — the word "tested" is part of the
phrase); bespoke scaffolding, evaluator accommodation, task leakage, and post
hoc task selection, each of which voids or downgrades per its gate.

**C.21 open-ended authoring.** *P1-forbidden as a result description at every
strength, including for the composite* (Charter-P1 §1.DG.3, §1.COMP.4; Ruling 8).
Defined here only as the composite **question** COMP-OEI = CI0 ∧ DG0 — clean-room
outsider authorship and disease-conserving generativity as *the same work*, not
two results filed adjacently. *Excludes:* discharge by either conjunct alone;
the prose name in any result-bearing sentence (cite the conjunction; reserve the
name for the question — P1's T10 mitigation, adopted by this draft `[PROPOSED]`).

**C.22 language succession; successor language.** A title conferred by a
tradition over time — sustained lineage, use, extensibility, influence.
*Exclusion, total (Charter-P1 Art. 13, carried whole):* succession **is not a
node**, is discharged by no node, composite, or combination, and no experiment
in this lab can grant it. In this repository "successor" may name a **research
trajectory** only where marked as intent (fork F-4 decides whether even that
marked use is permitted).

**C.23 fitness for latent-space machines.** A comparative empirical property:
under controlled, information-equivalent comparisons, model authors perform
measurably better with Lisp+ than with named alternatives. *Excludes:* design
intent — *"designed as an experimental language for latent-space authorship"* is
provenance and is licensed; *"for latent-space machines"* as a demonstrated
property is LM0's question and is unearned (its pre-registration skeleton is
explicitly not-to-be-run); memorization confounds — a campaign that cannot
distinguish "suits latent minds" from "matches our house style's training
footprint" tests the latter (§G.4).

## §G — Admission gates and failure semantics

**G.0 Standing of these gates.** Consolidation and nomenclature over existing
candidate machinery, not new law: the procedural bodies already exist as
candidates (PROTOCOL / ADJUDICATION / FAILURE-TAXONOMY / DG0-SKETCH /
LM0-PREREGISTRATION-SKELETON, all `-P1`, all unadopted; the clean-room brief
NOT ISSUABLE). Nothing below opens anything: **every gate here opens only by owner ruling.**
Precisely which bar holds each gate shut differs and is not flattened: the
jurisdictions of §G.1 and §G.2 are named in Ruling 6B's stop clause
(portability, conformance, independent-implementation, stranger audit, J2);
§G.3 is blocked derivatively (its J2 dependency sits behind that clause, and
T9 besides); §G.4 is named in no stop clause but is unopened, unpreregistered,
and — like every campaign after the 6B closure — the owner's to move
(*"Campaign holding; next movement is the owner's"*). Failure semantics are
mandatory clauses, not commentary. Two laws travel into all four gates from
standing instruments: **evaluator modification ⇒ no node claim** (P1 §9), and
**undisclosed exposure ⇒ VOID** (P1 §4.4).

### G.1 Independent Implementation /0 — the PortJ-L/0 gate (and what it is not)

- **Target and boundary:** the Ruling-3 two-target law governs. This gate is
  **PortJ-L/0 only**: a clean-room, non-CL implementation of the Many Acts /0
  *language layer* (the eleven-item work-list as both assignment and boundary),
  consuming the frozen Act Oracle Interface for substrate operations.
  PortJ-F/0 is a different gate that **cannot open** until the predecessor

- **Substrate distinctions (commission-required, adjudicated):** a second

  waiver cap); a second CL *implementation* (e.g. non-SBCL) = still same

- **Exposure fence:** implementer receives the sealed public packet only —
  frozen normative vectors, the AOI transcript, the issuable form of the brief
  — and nothing that has touched the reference implementation. Forbidden:
  reference source, its transcripts beyond the frozen packet, this
  repository's history. Fence verified *before* work begins; **undisclosed

- **Subject identity/independence:** axes per §D declared in advance;
  the author axis must be clean of the construction orbit (Sol is spent —

  be repaired in-campaign** (failure-taxonomy class 5 reports against J1);
  J2 evaluator-modification of the *canonical* judge is a category error that
  voids (nothing may touch J1's bytes).
- **Success threshold:** agreement across the full named frozen vector set,
  divergences classified per the failure taxonomy's five divergence classes
  (not this charter's CC classes).
- **Transcripts/custody:** both judges' observations in the normative
  observation format; hashes committed; custody chain whole.
- **Negative controls:** planted-divergence vectors must be caught (a
  comparator that has never failed is untested — the format's own §7 law).
- **Full success licenses:** the Ruling-3 green sentence, verbatim, with
  vector-set name — *within vector scope and the independence conditions
  actually met* — and never the short form.
- **Partial success licenses:** the axes and vector subsets actually crossed,
  named singly ("award only those axes").
- **Failure means:** evidence against *that implementation's* conformance.
- **Failure does NOT erase:** languagehood (rungs 1–3 stand or fall on their

  consulting reference internals improves interoperability and **does not

### G.4 Comparative Latent-Machine Authorship /0 — the LM0 gate

- **Ordering law (see §J):** runs LAST. Its measurements are uninterpretable
  while the only evaluator is construction-entangled and the only public
  materials are the house's own corpus.
- **Pre-registration:** the LM0 skeleton's five arms built in good faith
  (Lisp+; unrestricted CL; tagged JSON; a typed DSL; structured NL plans),
  preferably not by the hand that built arm 1; outcome measures with
  operational definitions; bands, falsifiers, equivalence margin (δ per the
  house law: a straddle-zero result is "not detected", never "null
  confirmed"), and task set frozen and hash-committed **before** any trial.
  *"Pre-registration is a precondition, not a courtesy."*
- **Confound controls (commission-required, made mandatory):**
  information-equivalent exposures across arms · model-family accounting on
  every subject · prompt and tool normalization · blinded adjudication where
  feasible · non-model baselines where relevant · and a **memorization
  control**: task sets constructed so success cannot be predicted by
  similarity to the published house corpus — else the campaign tests *"which
  model has memorized our house style?"* while advertising *"which language
  suits latent-space minds?"*, and must be reported as the former.
- **Absolute vs comparative, hard-separated:** absolute capability results
  (model X can author Lisp+) license nothing comparative; only pre-registered
  between-arm contrasts under the frozen analysis speak to suitability.
- **Full success licenses:** the pre-registered comparative sentence with its
  population, arms, margin, and task-class qualifiers — never "for
  latent-space machines" bare.
- **Partial success licenses:** the arms/tasks actually contrasted.
- **Failure means:** the comparative thesis is not detected at the tested
  size — designed-for provenance stands unchanged.
- **Failure does NOT erase:** languagehood, conformance, inhabitation, or
  generativity results — LM0 has no incoming edges and casts none backward.

## §J — Succession order (recommended, not commenced)

**J.0 Nothing herein opens anything.** Every campaign below sits behind
Ruling 6B's stop clause and its own gate; several sit behind unresolved forks.
This section recommends an order and exhibits the confound that justifies it.

**J.1 The recommended default order:**

1. **Charter /0 ratification** — after Sol's hostile jurisdictional review
   (under the shared-input caveat: Sol's approval would be a strong outside
   reading, never a second witness, and Sol's own P5/audit entanglements are
   part of what the review must not launder) and the owner's F-1…F-10
   dispositions.
2. **Public-sufficiency work** — not a numbered campaign but the standing
   precondition both fences depend on: the 28-place register worked down and a
   publication path ruled (SD-13). An exposure fence around insufficient
   public materials fences nothing.
3. **Independent Implementation /0** (as PortJ-L/0, §G.1).
4. **Independent Inhabitation /0** (CI0, §G.2).
5. **Generic Authoring /0** (DG0 → COMP-OEI, §G.3; requires F-7 first).
6. **Comparative Latent-Machine Authorship /0** (LM0, §G.4) — only then.

**J.2 The confound this order controls.** Comparative authorship evidence
gathered against a **sole, construction-entangled evaluator** cannot cleanly
distinguish *language comprehension* from *accommodation to that evaluator's
quirks*: a subject may succeed by learning the judge, not the language. A
second, independently seeded implementation (step 3) makes "the language, as
specified" and "the judge, as built" separable observables for the first time;
inhabitation (step 4) then tests the public materials on minds that could not
have absorbed the judge's quirks from the inside; generativity (step 5) tests
the mechanism rather than the specimen; and only under all of that does a
comparative campaign (step 6) measure a property of *the language* rather than
a property of *this repository*. Running 6 early would generate numbers whose
most parsimonious explanation is house-corpus familiarity — the exact
advertisement §G.4's memorization control forbids.

**J.3 Deviation trigger.** If the evidence ledger's dependency topology changes
(e.g., the owner rules a publication path that alters what "public materials"
means), the order is re-derived from the gates' preconditions, not preserved by
inertia.
