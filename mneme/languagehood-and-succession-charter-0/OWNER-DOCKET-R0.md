## F-1 — Which text governs as candidate charter?

**Question.** Two unadopted candidate charter texts now exist beside this one:
the frozen original (CHARTIST, historical as candidate text) and the Round-P
revision P1 (LATTICER, owner-directed structure per Rulings 1/3/8). R0 was
commissioned with a new prescribed structure. Which is the governing candidate
text going into hostile review and ratification?

- **Option A — R0 governs as working candidate; P1 preserved as
  historical-candidate stratum.** R0 subsumes P1's owner-ruled content
  (verified clause-for-clause in the correspondence digest) and adds the
  commission's instruments (status legend, independence matrix, claim grammar,
  gates, dockets).
- **Option B — P1 remains governing; R0 is filed as a commission response
  whose instruments are grafted into a P2 revision of P1.** Keeps the
  owner-directed lattice document primary; costs a second drafting round.
- **Option C — both stand as candidates addressed to different questions**
  (P1 = the campaign court's charter; R0 = the project-wide constitutional
  charter), cross-referencing without supersession.

**Evidence available.** P1's banner (supersedes the original as candidate text,
per Round-P authorization); Round P closed (Ruling 5A) with the bank
candidate-and-unadopted; the commission of 2026-08-11 prescribing R0's
structure without naming P1's disposition.

**Consequences.** A: one instrument to review and ratify; P1's unresolved
tensions (T8/T9/T10) travel into R0's docket (they do — F-7 carries T9; §C.21
carries T10's mitigation; T8 is inherited into rung 1's stratification note).
B: slower, but the owner-directed document stays the spine. C: two live
charters — highest drift risk; the correspondence table becomes permanent
maintenance.

**Recommendation.** **A**, with P1 marked HISTORICAL-as-candidate-text and
never deleted; any P1 clause R0 lacks remains citable at its own file.

**Default if deferred.** **B-shaped:** P1 remains the last owner-directed
candidate text; R0 waits as a commission response. (Conservative: the
owner-ruled structure outranks the newer draft until ruled otherwise.)

## F-2 — May a cross-lane status legend exist at all?

**Question.** Every index-class document at mneme level disclaims authority
over standing in its own text; no single page has ever assigned standing
tokens across lanes. R0's §A legend would be the first. Is that a gap being
filled or a line deliberately never crossed?

- **Option A — Yes, as a finding aid** carrying §A.2's disclaimers (records
  where standing was conferred and by whom; confers none).
- **Option B — No cross-lane legend**; standing is only ever read per-lane
  from each lane's own instruments.

**Evidence available.** `AUTHORITY-INDEX.md` ("Nothing in this index confers
standing"), `MANY-ACTS-0-STANDING.md` ("A banner cannot answer the question"),
`MANY-ACTS-0-SUPERSESSIONS.md` (path confers no standing); the practical cost
of their absence-of-summary: every reader re-derives standing from scattered
rulings, which is exactly how paraphrase-drift starts.

**Consequences.** A: one page to keep honest; if it rots it becomes the most
dangerous document in the repo (a stale legend *reads* authoritative). B: no
single point of failure; higher per-reader cost; drift risk moves into each
reader's private summary.

**Recommendation.** **A** — with a standing rule that the legend is re-derived
(not hand-edited) after every owner ruling, and a header stating its
generation source and date.

**Default if deferred.** **B.** The charter's §E statuses then read as this
document's classifications only, with no cross-lane force.

- **Option B — Ratify (ii) only**, leaving B.0 as standing commission language
  without adopted-law status.
- **Option C — Defer both** until the Many Acts /0 candidate is disposed, so

the compression without annotation remains CC-2. B: same for the

ratified; every languagehood sentence stays qualified by charter-candidacy

## F-4 — Is "successor" permitted as marked trajectory language?

**Question.** Article 13 (carried whole at C.22): succession is not a node and
no experiment can grant the title. May the word nonetheless appear as *marked
research-trajectory* language ("a trajectory toward a Lisp successor"), or is
it fully reserved?

- **Option A — Permit marked-trajectory use only**: the word may appear only
  inside an explicit intent frame ("trajectory toward", "aspires to"), never
  in a result sentence, never in a title of an evidence-bearing document.
- **Option B — Fully reserve the word**: it appears only in Article-13-style
  refusals.

**Evidence available.** Art. 13's rationale (a title conferred by tradition
over time); the commission's own §5 phrasing ("whether 'successor' is reserved
exclusively for an achieved rung or may also name a clearly marked research
trajectory"); rung 9's three-way collision showing how the word behaves when
compressed.

**Consequences.** A: honest ambition stays sayable; the marked frame is one
more qualifier that rider-7 discipline must keep attached. B: zero drift
risk; the project's own aspirational documents must paraphrase around their
actual aspiration, which breeds euphemism.

**Recommendation.** **A**, with the frame mandatory and greppable (the exact
phrase "trajectory toward" or "aspires to" within the same sentence).

**Default if deferred.** **B.**

- **Option A — Named intermediate standing** (LCI/0-pattern): "independently
  seeded under shared normative infrastructure, same substrate" — creditable
  as its own record, never toward C.15 substrate independence, never
  compressible to "second implementation".
- **Option B — No intermediate standing**: same-substrate work is preparation,
  recorded as candidate material only.

**Evidence available.** LCI/0 exists as the precedent (independently seeded,
same substrate, closure owner-authorized and internally audited, external pass
waived); PJ0's adopted gate expects an independently-seeded CL implementation
against the full vector set; Ruling 3's boundary (the AOI does not constitute
a second implementation of the full judge).

**Consequences.** A: real work earns a real, bounded name; risk is the
intermediate name compressing upward (mitigated by the mandatory long form).
B: contributors' same-substrate work is invisible in the lattice; the PJ0
gate's own vocabulary then has no charter home.

**Recommendation.** **A**, with the standing's full sentence fixed in the
ruling that grants it.

**Default if deferred.** **B.**
