## 6. Full findings

### SOL-HR-01 — Candidate authority is laundered into present law

**Severity:** RATIFICATION BLOCKER  
**Targets:** Charter §A.1; §C.19–C.22; §D.1 P5; §E.0, rungs 4, 5, 7–9; §F; §G.0; Succession Docket §0 and gate preambles; Claim Ceiling rows controlled only by P1.  
**Controlling rule:** Commission §2 source hierarchy; R0 §A.0–A.2; Evidence Ledger EV-41, which labels P1 candidate and unadopted.

R0 correctly records P1 as unadopted, then repeatedly lets P1 speak as though governing now. Examples include: ACCEPTED EVIDENCE is universally “terminal”; P1-forbidden strings are treated as presently forbidden; evaluator modification ⇒ no-node and undisclosed exposure ⇒ VOID are called laws from “standing instruments”; Sol is “permanently” spent; “open-ended” is forbidden at every strength; and no experiment can ever grant “successor.” Some of these rules may be excellent proposed constitutional law. Their quality does not confer standing.

**Consequence if unchanged:** the charter reproduces the exact hierarchy failure it condemns. A later reader cannot distinguish owner-ratified law, evidence-bounded present refusal, and R0’s proposed policy.

**Smallest lawful repair:** perform a clause-by-clause authority pass. For each P1-derived rule, do exactly one of three things:

1. cite an adopted owner ruling that independently establishes it;
2. mark it `[PROPOSED]` and place it in CC-3 / the owner docket;
3. restate it as a present evidential ceiling rather than permanent law.

Fable may perform the demotions mechanically. Owner disposition is required only to adopt the surviving proposals. This finding affects evidence standing and claim grammar, not the underlying empirical records.

### SOL-HR-02 — The promised status system does not classify its own propositions

**Severity:** RATIFICATION BLOCKER  
**Targets:** Charter §A.1–A.1a; §E rungs 1–3; Claim Ceiling lines for languagehood and distinctness.  
**Controlling rule:** Charter §A.1: every classified proposition carries exactly one of six statuses.

Rung 1 uses `SPLIT`; rungs 1–2 use “ADOPTED-stratum witnessed” and “candidate-witnessed”; rung 3 uses a mixture of “licensed,” external-only, and ratifiable. None is one of the six statuses. These phrases are useful **support annotations**, but they are not proposition statuses. The same page explicitly distinguishes proposition status from source standing, then quietly lets the categories fraternize in the corridor.

**Consequence if unchanged:** a mechanical reader cannot determine whether the project-wide languagehood proposition is ADOPTED LAW, ACCEPTED EVIDENCE, PROPOSED HOLDING, or something else.

**Smallest lawful repair:** give every rung one primary §A status and move strata into a separate `Support / standing` field. Recommended mapping:

- rungs 1–3: **PROPOSED HOLDING**, supported at adopted-seam scale by adopted sources and at program scale by candidate material;
- rungs 4–5: **ACCEPTED EVIDENCE** at their exact sentences;
- rungs 6–8 and 10: **OPEN**, with the existing unopened/jurisdiction-closed subannotation;
- rung 9: **REFUSED CLAIM under current evidence** for the composite sentence, while any permanent succession doctrine remains `[PROPOSED]` until ruled.

No owner decision is needed to repair the grammar. This is candidate coherence and claim classification.

### SOL-HR-04 — Languagehood is ratifiable, but the definition and proof route need sharpening

**Severity:** MAJOR  
**Targets:** Charter B.0–B.5; C.6, C.8; §E rungs 1–3; §F.1 items 1–2; EV-15/45/46; F-3.  
**Controlling rule:** Commission §§1.5, 5.B–E.

C.6 admits any notation with syntax, validation, refusals, operational behavior, and an observable result. A finite command protocol, message schema, or elaborate API can satisfy that floor without being a language in the intended sense. R0 needs a compositional or rule-governed interpretation criterion: structured expressions must combine under semantic rules, not merely select endpoints.

Conversely, “hosted/embedded” does not require a prior lab document to have uttered the adjective. It is an analytic classification of the host relationship. EV-15’s negative grep and EV-45/46’s slice-scope vocabulary are historical terminology notes, not evidence that whole-Lisp+ is or is not hosted. Slice /0 can illustrate the enforcement-perimeter distinction; it cannot carry whole-language weight.

B.0 itself survives, provided it states what happens when specification and implementation diverge: the implementation does not silently become law, but neither does a candidate, silent, or contradictory specification yield an adopted answer.

**Consequence if unchanged:** C.6 over-includes trivial protocols while rung 3 under-grounds an otherwise defensible hosted-language classification in lexical precedent.

**Smallest lawful repair:** use the replacement language in §7 below; make EV-15/45/46 non-load-bearing; split F-3 into jurisdiction and classification holdings. Owner disposition is required to ratify the holdings, not to repair the analysis.

### SOL-HR-05 — P5’s independence coordinates are wrong

**Severity:** MAJOR  
**Targets:** Charter C.15, C.18, D.1–D.2; Evidence Ledger EV-14/21–23.  
**Controlling rule:** Commission §5.D; Charter C.15 and C.18 themselves.

P5’s `Substrate = YES` is a category error. P5 was authored by a model on another model substrate, but it executed through the same Lisp+ judge; no alternate implementation substrate carried the semantics. Under C.15’s own definition, the cell is **N/A** or **NOT TESTED**, not YES.

`Model-family = YES (GPT lineage vs Claude lineage)` also contradicts C.18’s rule that provider/product difference does not prove family independence. The archive supplies brand lineage, not a claim-relative training-provenance argument. The correct cell is **NOT TESTED** (or PARTIAL only if authenticated provenance beyond brand is added).

The topology also loses a useful distinction between implementation language and runtime implementation. A second independently coded Common Lisp judge tested on CCL rather than SBCL does not establish different-language substrate independence, but it does test runtime portability. That should be recorded as a subordinate profile, not collapsed into “same substrate, nothing.”

**Smallest lawful repair:** rescore P5 as Author PARTIAL; Implementation N/A; Substrate N/A/NOT TESTED; Model-family NOT TESTED; Audit NO. Add an explicit runtime-variation annotation or subaxis. No owner decision is needed to correct P5; F-6 can later decide the name of any intermediate implementation standing.

### SOL-HR-06 — “Terminus” and “spent independence” prohibit too much

**Severity:** MAJOR  
**Targets:** §A.1 ACCEPTED EVIDENCE; §D.1 P5; §E.0 and rungs 4–5; Evidence Ledger dependency topology; Claim Ceiling R-P4/R-P5.  
**Controlling rule:** R-P4 and R-P5 exact ceilings; Ruling 8’s non-smuggling structure; claim-relative independence in C.18.

R-P4 and R-P5 must not accumulate into CI0, portability, or any other named claim. That does not mean they “create no edges” in every evidential sense or are “not two witnesses to anything.” They are distinct, construction-entangled experiments supporting different narrow propositions. They may inform gate design, expose defects, and provide dependent background evidence without becoming independent corroboration or installments toward a node.

Likewise, prior exposure is permanent as a historical fact but disqualification is claim-relative. Sol cannot later satisfy a fence whose criterion requires no access to materials Sol has already seen. That does not make Sol universally “spent” for every future artifact, extension, or unrelated audit question.

**Smallest lawful repair:** replace universal terminality with a **no-promotion/no-aggregation rule**, and scope exposure disqualification to named materials and claims. This can be repaired without owner action unless the owner wishes to ratify the generalized rule.

### SOL-HR-07 — LM0 has an undeclared incoming edge

**Severity:** MAJOR

**Controlling rule:** §E.0’s definition: an edge means a claim/campaign cannot be attempted until another condition is met.

The charter declares no incoming LM0 edges, then makes completion or waiver of earlier campaigns an admission condition. Under its own definition, that is an incoming procedural edge. “No evidential implication” and “no procedural prerequisite” are different propositions; R0 currently uses one sentence for both.

**Smallest lawful repair:** either:

- retain no incoming edges, demote “runs last” to a rebuttable recommendation, and remove it from the admission checklist.

My recommendation is the first. Owner disposition is required only if this ordering is to be adopted as policy.

### SOL-HR-08 — PortJ-L/0 can be passed by memorizing the public vectors

**Severity:** MAJOR  
**Targets:** §G.1; Succession Docket §1.  
**Controlling rule:** Commission §5.G.1; the gate’s intended claim of an evaluator rather than a specimen lookup table.

An independently coded non-CL program can hard-code the entire public frozen vector set or dispatch on vector identities, pass every named vector, and satisfy the current text. Planted divergences test the comparator, not whether J2 implements a general semantic relation. A full public-vector pass is therefore coverage evidence but not enough to support the intended “implementation” noun.

**Smallest lawful repair:** add all of the following to the gate:

- preregistered unseen holdout vectors or post-freeze generated/metamorphic cases not exposed to the implementer;
- a structural anti-special-casing review proving J2 interprets arbitrary well-formed forms within the declared grammar rather than matching vector IDs;
- metamorphic relations that vary irrelevant names/order/content while preserving or predictably changing semantics;
- separate first-run, repaired, and reference-consulted dispositions;
- negative controls that test both comparator sensitivity and implementation generalization without revealing the implementation strategy;
- explicit reporting of implementation-language variation and runtime variation as different profiles.

The hidden set must itself be governed and custody-sealed; Ruling 6B must be reopened before any such object is created. No campaign is authorized by this recommendation.

### SOL-HR-09 — CI0 can pass on a trivial or task-leaked specimen

**Severity:** MAJOR  
**Targets:** §G.2; Succession Docket §2; F-10.  
**Controlling rule:** Commission §5.G.2.

“Genuinely unseen program” and “compose beyond a curated specimen” are not operationalized. The cheapest pass is a task structurally isomorphic to a public example, selected by construction hands, with one trivial valid program. The clarification rule blocks direct coaching but not task selection leakage, house-corpus similarity, retry cherry-picking, or a documentation repair followed by reuse of the now-exposed author.

The disposition “green execution + wrong prediction = semantic transmission did not” is also too strong. One wrong prediction shows that prediction failed and that no transmission result was established for that item; it does not prove categorical non-transmission.

**Smallest lawful repair:** preregister task generation, novelty/topology criteria, selection custody, tool access, retry count, full interaction transcript, and success thresholds for each of the six capacities. Require the task selector to be outside the construction loop or blind to candidate identity. Treat any author exposed during a documentation-repair cycle as ineligible for a fresh clean-room first pass on the repaired packet. Replace the wrong-prediction sentence as in §7.

### SOL-HR-10 — DG0 can manufacture diversity by changing labels

**Severity:** MAJOR  
**Targets:** §G.3; Succession Docket §3; F-7/F-10.  
**Controlling rule:** Commission §5.G.3.

“Materially distinct topology” is undefined. Three domains with the same control-flow, authority/evidence graph, and refusal structure can be renamed into three “topology classes.” The attempt registry is also not mechanically frozen, so unreported failures remain hard to detect. Conserving inherited diseases is necessary but not sufficient: new topology can introduce new ways to violate the law while every old disease remains red.

The text also says DG0 is author-agnostic and authorship unconstrained while requiring more than one author lineage. These can coexist only if it states that independence is irrelevant to DG0’s claim but sample diversity is still required.

**Smallest lawful repair:** preregister topology equivalence features—e.g. act-dependency graph, branch/join shape, binding/visibility structure, authority/evidence flow, refusal-path classes, and extension mechanism—and have class assignment adjudicated before outcomes. Hash-commit every attempted task, not only reported successes. Add new mutation controls for each new topology/extension. Clarify author-agnostic versus diversity. Defer numeric floors until DG0 opening.

### SOL-HR-11 — LM0’s comparison is not yet a comparison of languages

**Severity:** MAJOR  
**Targets:** §G.4; Succession Docket §4; LM0 skeleton references.  
**Controlling rule:** Commission §5.G.4.

The five arms differ not only in notation but in expressivity, tooling, evaluation semantics, documentation maturity, and error feedback. “Information-equivalent exposure” does not make unrestricted Common Lisp, tagged JSON, a typed DSL, and structured natural-language plans commensurable. The current “preferably not” separation of arm builder and preregistrar and “blinded where feasible” are invitations to convenient exceptions.

The statistical plan is also underspecified. A declared analysis is not enough without a preregistered estimand, experimental unit, randomization/blocking, repeated-measure structure, sample size/power or precision target, multiplicity policy, equivalence/noninferiority margins, missingness rules, and model/task random effects where applicable.

The memorization control cannot prove absence of training exposure. It can only reduce and measure recognizable house-corpus similarity; residual uncertainty must remain in the claim.

**Smallest lawful repair:** define a common semantic task envelope and equivalent observable requirements across arms; separate language effect from tooling/evaluator effect; require builder/preregistrar/adjudicator separation rather than prefer it; preregister the full statistical design; use post-freeze, style-decoupled tasks with lexical/structural similarity audits and residual-confound disclosure; and predeclare how failure of equivalence or blinding downgrades the claim.

## 7. Exact proposed replacement wording

The following language is proposed for R0.1. It does not adopt itself.

### W-01 — Status grammar

> **Primary status and support annotation.** Every classified proposition receives exactly one primary status from the six-token legend. Source standing, evidential support, stratum, and jurisdiction state are separate annotations and never substitute for that primary status.

### W-02 — Semantic jurisdiction doctrine

> **[PROPOSED] Within the scope of each adopted Lisp+ specification, the host executes the judge but does not supply the normative meaning of Lisp+ forms. Where the applicable specification is candidate, silent, underdetermined, or contradictory, no adopted answer may be inferred from the reference implementation alone; the condition is recorded as candidate standing or specification defect and requires the appropriate owner process.**

### W-03 — Languagehood definition

> **Languagehood** is the bounded property of a notation whose structured expressions are governed by specification-owned well-formedness and a nontrivial, rule-governed semantic relation that composes expression structure—such as binding, sequencing, branching, authority/evidence relations, or refusal conditions—into observable consequences. **Excluded:** a finite message schema, command vocabulary, or API whose forms merely select host operations without an independently stated compositional or interpretive semantics; and every separate claim of portability, implementation independence, inhabitation, generality, succession, or population fitness.

### W-04 — Exact ratifiable classification

> **[PROPOSED] On the presently accepted record, Lisp+ warrants classification as a small experimental hosted language: adopted One Act /0 and Surface Account /0 establish a specification-owned language kernel, while the Many Acts /0 candidate supplies bounded program-scale evidence of composition, binding, branching, typed refusal, and observable consequence. This classification does not adopt the Many Acts /0 candidate and does not establish portable conformance, an independent implementation, clean-room outsider inhabitation, general or open-ended authoring, succession, or comparative fitness for model authors.**

### W-05 — Hosted-language definition

> **Hosted language** describes an implementation relation: the language’s reader, judge, or runtime executes inside a host system while the applicable language specification, not ordinary host evaluation, supplies normative meaning. The classification does not imply host-level closure, tamper resistance, portability, or implementation independence.

### W-06 — Accepted-evidence non-aggregation

> **Accepted evidence is citable only at its recorded proposition and ceiling. It does not accumulate, by addition or rhetorical promotion, to discharge another named claim. It may inform gate design, defect analysis, and dependent background reasoning, provided that use is identified as construction-entangled and is never counted as independent corroboration.**

### W-07 — Claim-relative exposure

> **Exposure is permanent as historical provenance and disqualifies a subject from any future clean-room claim whose fence excludes materials that subject has already seen. It does not create a universal, context-free status of being “spent”; eligibility is assessed against the named claim, materials, and construction orbit.**

### W-08 — Corrected P5 matrix row

> **R-P5:** Author independence **PARTIAL** (non-implementation author, construction/adjudication-orbit exposed); Implementation independence **N/A**; Substrate independence **NOT TESTED** (the canonical Lisp+ judge remained the only implementation); Model-family independence **NOT TESTED** (named product/provider difference is insufficient provenance); Audit independence **NO**.

### W-09 — Rung 9

> **Status: REFUSED CLAIM under current evidence.** “Lisp+ qualifies as an independently implemented Lisp successor” compresses an unearned implementation claim, an unresolved independence topology, and a succession title into one sentence. No present source licenses it. **[PROPOSED]** Whether “successor” is permanently non-empirical or may appear only as marked research trajectory is an owner policy question; R0 does not settle that question by citing an unadopted predecessor charter.

### W-10 — Cross-gate candidate terms

> **[PROPOSED GATE TERM] Unless an adopted ruling already supplies the rule, evaluator modification, undisclosed exposure, task leakage, or reference-source consultation receives only the disposition expressly defined by the owner-adopted campaign protocol. R0 proposes the void/no-node dispositions below; it does not represent them as adopted before that protocol is ruled.**

### W-11 — Wrong semantic prediction

> **A green execution with an incorrect frozen prediction establishes execution success for that item but failure of semantic prediction; it licenses no semantic-transmission result. It does not, by itself, prove that the public materials transmitted no semantics.**

### W-12 — Universal failure and backward-impeachment clause

> **Failure does not automatically erase any lower finding. If the failure is traced to a contradiction, omission, invalid oracle, defective witness, or other defect in a lower finding’s own premises or evidence, the campaign must open a backward-impeachment docket naming the affected finding and source. Adoption status remains until owner process changes it; evidential criticism is neither suppressed nor converted into automatic repeal.**

### W-13 — LM0 procedural edge

> **LM0 has no evidential incoming edges: no earlier result makes its comparative thesis true. It does carry a procedural admission precondition: the implementation, public-sufficiency, inhabitation, and generativity confounds identified in §J must either be addressed by completed work or expressly waived by owner ruling with the residual confound written into the licensed sentence. This procedural edge transmits no evidence.**

### W-14 — Negative existence claims

Replace bare universals such as “no second implementation exists” with:

> **No second implementation of the Lisp+ judge is recorded in the authoritative sources directly authenticated for this charter at the named commit.**

Replace “the stranger audit has never occurred on any lane” with:

> **No stranger-audit report is recorded in the directly authenticated lane records named by this charter; the One Act /0 gate was waived, not passed.**
