  WHAT THE SECOND TABLE RECORDS.  The chair's first aliasing harness
  aliased the accessor's RETURN and not the datum's INTERNAL STORE.
  Under it, the shipped [46] appeared to discriminate — which would have
  made the entire hollow-tooth finding a false alarm.  It was caught only
  because its answer disagreed with a report the chair had reason to trust.

  A SIMULATION STANDING IN FOR THE THING SIMULATED, inside the session
  whose subject is exactly that.  It is preserved here as a live control,
  not as an anecdote.

  WHY THE SHIPPED FORM COULD NOT FAIL.  It filled the FIRST read, then
  compared the store against a datum rebuilt from a SECOND read.  Under
  an aliasing accessor BOTH SIDES ARE THE SAME ZEROED VECTOR, so equality
  holds.  It demonstrated that MAKE-BYTES-DATUM . BYTES-DATUM-VALUE round
  trips.  Its label claimed it demonstrated defensive copying.

  THE STANDING OF THE REPAIRED FORM.  It is a TRIPWIRE ON AN IMPORTED
  GUARANTEE, not Form /1 coverage.  CD/0's copy-on-access is pinned by
  CD/0's own suite in CD/0's own language and in the Python seed; if it
  ever regressed, CD/0's suite is what goes red.  [46] exists so that
  Form /1 does not keep printing green while the threat model beneath it
  has changed.
