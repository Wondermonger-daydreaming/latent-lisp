FORM /1 IDENTITY INJECTIVITY — the census the sensitivity suite never ran
██████████████████████████████████████████████████████████████████████████████

THE CLAIM
  Form /1 identity values are injective over their declared CD/0 phase
  payloads, assuming Canonical Datum /0 exact encoding.

WHAT IS NOT CLAIMED
  Injectivity over unrecorded live host facts.  Two contexts with identical
  declarations and different live anchors produce the SAME identity by
  design; that is the declared residual (NC-31B), not a collision.  This
  census generates such pairs on purpose and counts them separately.

NO HASH IS ADDED.  CD/0 supplies no digest.  An identity here is a LOSSLESS
  CANONICAL ENCODING that contains its payload, which is why the round trip
  in PART A is a proof of injectivity over the population it covers, and
  why PART B searches the one thing the round trip cannot settle: whether
  two DIFFERENT declared configurations can reach the SAME payload.
