## 3. What this lane does NOT claim, and its named limits

**Crash model at exact size:** recovery after uncatchable SIGKILL while
the OS, kernel, filesystem, and storage stack remain alive. **NOT
established:** power-loss durability · hardware-cache durability ·
storage-controller failure tolerance · arbitrary filesystem crash
tolerance · torn-sector tolerance · cross-filesystem guarantees ·
cross-platform determinism · live-provider conformance · distributed
correctness. Journal /0's truncation/corruption fixtures remain separate
evidence, not fused here. Successful sync witnessing earns only *"the
implementation invoked its declared durability protocol before the
process-death boundary."*

**Not audited, not adopted, not frozen, not on a governing floor.** No
hostile review, stranger audit, fresh-weights audit,
primitive-minimization audit, freeze or promotion ceremony was
commissioned from this lane (per the ruling). The words "independently
verified/validated" are used of nothing here (AP0 rider 2 stands).

Named limits and honest edges (none silent):

1. **`occupied target before effect` — apparent missing predecessor
   predicate (§15 class, reported not repaired).** The composed stack
   has no refusal for a second authorized attempt on an already-written
   cell: Capability /2 settles last-write-wins and does not expose
   Kernel /0's occupied `perform`. No seat of this specimen requires
   it; the §14 control is recorded NOT EXERCISED and excluded from the
   live counter (32, not 33). Chair ruling: out of scope for this
   specimen; the missing law is owed to the upstream docket.
2. **Witness scope:** `check-sync-before-every-ready` proves
   last-write < last-fsync < READY per boundary — which any earlier
   synced append can satisfy; the marker-before-sync control therefore
   uses tight corpse-absence + per-boundary fsync-count legs instead.
   The shipped check is not wrong; it is weaker than its name. A
   per-boundary binding is recommended for a future witness revision.
3. **Reconstruction children are not straced.** The oracle-separation
   read-set proof covers campaign and control children; the
   reconstruction/replay programs' oracle-independence rests on their
   census-only load chain, its live guard (fired in-gate), and the
   byte-equality of four independent derivations.
4. **O8 (missing-cost-as-zero) is killed predicate-named, not
   condition-typed** — the budget fold's declared clause is asserted
   verbatim; a typed condition would change green behavior and was not
   added.
5. **A declared diagnostic escape hatch** exists in the official
   reconstruction program (`VERTICAL0_RECONSTRUCT_DIAGNOSTIC_TOOTH`,
   required by the §11 in-process tooth); child environments strip every
   `VERTICAL0_*` variable, and the guard's refusal is itself gate-fired.
6. **Latent path, recorded not repaired:** the streaming-seat resume
   logic would re-enter a completed streaming seat (keys on
   chunk-1 ∧ ¬closure, no consumption check). Unreachable in the sealed
   campaign; noted for any successor.
7. **The oracle is committed and public** — separation is enforced by
   the read-set witness at runtime, not by secrecy.
8. **S2/S12's request-loss is a declared simulation** (cap2's
   `:request-lost` seam, public config): the fake provider "loses" those
   requests deterministically; from the durable bytes the standing is
   genuine `:crossed-unsettled`. The SIGKILL composes with — never
   substitutes for — that simulation.
9. **The harness run-root is fixed, not parameterized** (the literal
   `runs/campaign-1/`, which campaign mode recreates by deletion).
