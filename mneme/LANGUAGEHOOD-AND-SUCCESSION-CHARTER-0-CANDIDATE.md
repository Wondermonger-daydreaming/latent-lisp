## Article 0 — What this charter is, and what it is not

**0.1 Purpose.** This charter fixes, in advance, the claim ladder for Lisp+ languagehood
and succession: what each station means, what evidence discharges it, and what sentence
becomes licensed when it is discharged. Its function is negative before it is positive —
it exists to make **evidence migrating upward without authorization** a detectable act
rather than a rhetorical drift.

**0.2 Prospective only.** This charter governs **succession claims made after its
adoption**. It does not rewrite, re-grade, or retroactively re-license any previously
adopted finding. Where a previously adopted record used different vocabulary, that record
stands as sealed; this charter binds the *next* claim, not the old one.

**0.3 Its own standing.** This document is a candidate. Nothing in it is adopted by being
written, by being reviewed, by being agreed with, or by any test turning green. Article 11
governs.

**0.4 Not a schedule.** Naming a station is not a plan to reach it, an estimate that it is
reachable, or a promise that reaching it is desirable. A station may stand unearned
forever without embarrassing the project.

## Article 1 — The stations

Five stations. Each carries: **status**, **discharge condition**, **the licensed
sentence** (the *only* form in which the station may be asserted), and **forbidden

### L0 — Specification-owned languagehood

**Status: EARNED** (subject to Article 11 ratification of *this charter*; the underlying
evidence is a mix of adopted and candidate material — see 1.L0.4).

**1.L0.1 What it asserts.** Lisp+ forms have normative meaning governed by the Lisp+
specification and its judge, even though Common Lisp executes the implementation. The
five properties, stated separately because each is separately witnessed:

1. **Specification-owned syntax and validation** — a class of well-formed programs and a
   method of rejecting malformed ones, terminating before consequential activity.
2. **Operational meaning not conferred by host evaluation** — an MA0 form is *data* to
   Common Lisp; CL's evaluation rules do not determine what it means.
3. **Refusal behavior as a first-class semantic result**, not an error convention.
4. **Observable adjudicative consequences** — distinctions between executions that the
   specification, not the host, defines.
5. **Semantic jurisdiction distinct from CL evaluation** — the substrate executes the
   judge; it is not itself the law.

**1.L0.2 The licensed sentence.**

> Lisp+ programs possess specification-owned syntax and semantics: their normative
> meaning is defined by the Lisp+ specification and adjudicated by its judge, and is not
> inherited from Common Lisp evaluation.

Equivalently, at the chair's compression: *Lisp+ is a small experimental language.*

**1.L0.3 Forbidden sentences.** "Common Lisp cannot express Lisp+ semantics" (the
category error corrected by Sol — CL implements the evaluator, so expressibility is not
and never was the criterion; **jurisdiction** is). "Lisp+ is a new Lisp." "Lisp+ is a
successor to Lisp" (Article 13). Any sentence that converts finiteness into a virtue
claim: L0 is compatible with a language of severely bounded expressivity, and says
nothing about generality.

**1.L0.4 Evidence base, stated honestly.** L0 rests on **adopted** material (One Act /0,
`language-act-0/ADOPTION-RECORD-2026-08-08.md`) *and* on **candidate** material (Many Acts
/0 and its R1 repair, which are candidates only). The station is therefore earned **at
mixed standing**: the seam is adopted, the program-as-data evaluator over it is not. Any
citation of L0 that leans on MA0 evidence must carry the word *candidate*. If the owner
declines the Many Acts /0 candidate, L0's evidence base contracts to the adopted seam and
must be restated at that smaller size.

### IH0 — Bounded stranger inhabitation

**Status: NOT EARNED at its own name.** Two *sub-thresholds beneath it* are discharged;
neither is stranger inhabitation, and their sum is not stranger inhabitation.

**1.IH0.1 What the station asserts when earned.** A mind **outside the construction and
adjudication orbit of the project** authors a program from public materials, and that
program is accepted and behaves as its author intended, without evaluator modification.

**1.IH0.2 Sub-threshold A — P4 (same-author post-freeze holdout).** Earned, exactly:

> A same-author, post-R1-freeze holdout program was expressible through the repaired
> candidate authoring surface without evaluator modification.

Witness: P4 "vindemia", green first execution, 11 checks / 0 failures, zero post-freeze
changes to grammar, evaluator, validator, or fixtures. **P4 contains no inhabitation
content whatsoever.** Its author is the author of the grammar. It tests *evaluator
genericity against a genuinely later program*, which is a different property from
authorship by another. It may not be described as partial, weak, or proto-inhabitation.

**1.IH0.3 Sub-threshold B — P5 (non-implementation author, different substrate).** The
governing claim is the owner's provenance correction, appended verbatim to the frozen P5
protocol (`notes/2026-08-10-p5-sol-inhabitation-protocol.md`). Quoted here in full and
quotable nowhere else in shorter form:

> A post-freeze holdout program authored on a different model substrate by a
> non-implementation author, with disclosed prior source/transcript exposure and
> advisory participation, was accepted through the frozen public candidate surface and
> behaved exactly as predicted, without evaluator modification.

What P5 earned, itemized: a non-author program, expressed through the frozen candidate
surface; evaluator, validator, grammar and fixtures unmodified; 10 checks / 0 failures on
first execution; and **every asserted prediction matched** — disposition, refusal code,
nested detail verbatim, summary count, summary order, summary dispositions, and arm D
witnessed unexecuted. The exact prediction match is **cross-substrate semantic-agreement
evidence**.

**1.IH0.4 What P5 is NOT, by owner ruling.** Not stranger inhabitation. Not independent
inhabitation. Not guide-only semantic transmission (prior exposure means the public guide
cannot be isolated as the sole transmission channel). Not an independent implementation —
and it does **not** become one by virtue of the authoring having occurred on another model
substrate. Substrate difference is not orbit difference.

**1.IH0.5 Forbidden sentences.** "Authored against the public author guide" without an
exposure qualifier · "the first program by a mind outside the construction loop" ·
"guide-only transmission" · "independent inhabitation" · "stranger" applied to any P5
artifact or actor · any sentence in which P4 and P5 are summed into a single inhabitation
claim.

**1.IH0.6 Discharge condition for the station proper.** An author who (a) did not build,
review, adjudicate, repair, or advise the implementation; (b) had no access to
implementation source, repair witnesses, execution transcripts, or holdout programs; (c)
worked from published materials only; and (d) whose exposure is disclosed and auditable —
authors a program that is accepted and behaves as they predicted, without evaluator
modification. One such author is an **existence proof**, not a general result, and must be
reported as one.

### PJ/0-portable — Portable conformance

*First use, collision note: **Portable Judge /0 — abbreviated here PJ/0-portable; the bare
token PJ0 remains reserved for the adopted Process Journal /0; final designation pending
owner ratification.***

**Status: NOT EARNED.**

**1.PJ.1 What the station asserts when earned.** An **independently seeded** implementation
of the Lisp+ judge, on a different substrate, agrees with the canonical judge on the full
normative vector set — public *and* hidden — with every divergence adjudicated to
specification text rather than to either implementation's behavior.

**1.PJ.2 Kinship and distinctness.** This station is a deliberate sibling of the adopted
Process Journal /0's binding gate **R-PJ-3** (`architecture/process-journal-0/
PJ0-ADOPTION-RECORD.md`, sealed 2026-07-18), which holds that the PJ0 packet's
verification is *self-consistency certification*, with no stronger conformance claim and
no specimen reliance permitted until an independently-seeded CL implementation passes the
full vector set. **They are not the same gate.** R-PJ-3 governs the *Process Journal
specification's* serialization conformance; PJ/0-portable governs the *language judge's*
normative agreement. Discharging one does not discharge the other, and a document may not
cite R-PJ-3 as though it were PJ/0-portable or vice versa. The naming collision is
structural, not merely lexical (both live under `mneme/`), which is exactly why the bare
token `PJ0` stays reserved.

**1.PJ.3 The licensed sentence, when earned.**

> An independently seeded second implementation of the Lisp+ judge agreed with the
> canonical judge on the frozen normative vector set; the divergences observed were N,
> each adjudicated to specification text.

Divergence count is **part of the sentence**, always. A conformance run with zero
divergences and a conformance run with three adjudicated divergences are different
results and are reported differently.

**1.PJ.4 Forbidden sentences.** "Independently verified" and "independently validated" are
**forbidden wording** (house rider, binding). "Portable language" without naming the
vector set and its freeze. "Second implementation" without the independence provenance of
Article 4. Any claim that portability establishes usability, inhabitation, generality, or
model benefit.

### OG0 — Disease-conserving generativity

**Status: NOT EARNED.**

**1.OG.1 What the station asserts when earned.** Outsider authors construct **materially
differently topologized, previously unseen applications** through a **declared public
extension mechanism**, without evaluator modification, and **without turning a single
inherited disease witness green**.

**1.OG.2 The licensed phrase, exactly.**

> evidence of disease-conserving generativity across unseen application topologies

**1.OG.3 The bound that makes it honest.** **Finite experiments cannot prove literal
open-endedness.** No number of new applications establishes that the language can grow
indefinitely; each run supplies *evidence across the topologies actually tested* and
nothing beyond them. The words "open-ended", "unbounded", "arbitrary domains", and
"general-purpose" are forbidden as descriptions of any OG0 result.

**1.OG.4 The conserved quantity.** Article 8 is OG0's mechanical body: an extension is
lawful only if every prior disease still turns its witness **red, unmodified**. A door
reopened is a disease whose witness went quiet. Generativity purchased by subtracting an
impossibility is not generativity; it is a smaller language wearing a larger vocabulary.

**1.OG.5 Dependency, declared.** OG0 as commissioned requires **outsider** authors, and
therefore cannot be discharged before IH0's station-proper condition (1.IH0.6) is
satisfiable — the ladder's numbering is not a claim that OG0 sits above PJ/0-portable in
difficulty, only that it is a distinct question. See Article 2: order is not implication.

**Status: NOT EARNED. Thesis under test.**

**1.LM.1 What the station asserts when earned.** Under controlled, information-equivalent
comparisons, multiple model authors perform **measurably better** with Lisp+ than with
each named alternative: unrestricted Common Lisp, tagged JSON workflow descriptions, a
typed DSL, and structured natural-language plans.

**1.LM.2 The only licensed sentence until then.**

> Lisp+ is designed as an experimental language for latent-space authorship.

**1.LM.3 Forbidden sentences, until discharged.** "Lisp+ has been shown to be better" ·

## Article 2 — Non-substitutability

**2.1 The stations are non-substitutable.** Each answers a different question; none is a
weaker or stronger version of another; evidence discharging one **never partially
discharges** another.

Named consequences, each a real drift this charter exists to block:

- **Portable conformance does not earn OG0.** A second implementation agreeing on the
  vectors says nothing about whether new applications can be grown.
- **OG0 does not establish model benefit.** Outsiders growing new lawful domains says
  nothing about whether models author them *better* than they would author JSON.
- **P5 does not become independent implementation** because the authored program
  originated on another substrate. Authoring a program is not implementing a judge, and a
  different model substrate is not an independent orbit.
- **L0 does not imply anything above it.** Specification-owned meaning is compatible with
  a language nobody outside the project can use, that no second implementation realizes,
  and that no model benefits from.
- **IH0's sub-thresholds do not sum.** P4 (same-author) + P5 (non-implementation author
  with disclosed exposure) ≠ stranger inhabitation, at any weighting.

**2.2 Order is not implication.** The ladder's presentation order is expository. A later
station may be attempted first; a later station may be discharged while an earlier one
stands open. Nothing may be inferred about station X from the state of station Y except
where a dependency is declared explicitly (1.OG.5).

**2.3 Partial discharge is a finding, not a fraction.** A station is earned or it is not.
Evidence short of the discharge condition is recorded as a **named sub-threshold with its
own licensed sentence** (as 1.IH0.2 and 1.IH0.3 are), never as a percentage, never as
"nearly", never as "on track".

## Article 3 — Freezing a normative seed

**3.1** Before any conformance, inhabitation, or generativity campaign opens, the
governing artifacts are **frozen and identified by commit and tree hash**: the
specification text, the vector set (public portion published, hidden portion sealed), the
disease corpus, and the public authoring materials.

**3.2** The freeze is the campaign's reference point. From the freeze until the campaign's
verdict, the frozen artifacts are **untouchable** — not corrected, not clarified, not
improved, not annotated in place.

**3.3** A defect discovered in a frozen artifact mid-campaign is **preserved and reported**
as a finding of that campaign; it is not repaired inside it. Repair belongs to the next
freeze (Article 7).

**3.4** A campaign whose seed was not frozen before it opened yields **no station claim**,
whatever its outcome. The result may be reported as exploratory; it may not promote.

## Article 4 — Implementer independence

**4.1 Independence is partly a PROVENANCE claim, not only a code claim.** Whether an
implementation is independent is settled by *who wrote it and what they had seen*, not by
inspecting the artifact.

**4.2 The disqualifying relations.** An implementation is **not** independent if its
author (a) participated in the construction loop of the canonical implementation; (b)
participated in adjudication, review, repair, or advisory analysis of it; (c) had access
to canonical implementation source, repair witnesses, execution transcripts, or holdout
programs; or (d) imported, ported, transliterated, or consulted canonical implementation
code.

**4.3 The named consequence.** **A second evaluator written in another language by a
construction-loop participant is a PORT, not a clean-room witness.** Language difference
is not independence. Substrate difference is not independence. A rewrite by the same mind
is one mind's reading twice.

**4.4 Disclosure is mandatory and is data, not disqualification-by-itself.** Every
implementer files an exposure statement before the run. An undisclosed exposure discovered
afterward **voids the conformance claim** for that campaign; a disclosed exposure is
recorded in the licensed sentence as a qualifier.

**4.5 Standing example, filed so it cannot be forgotten.** Sol authored the adopted
Process Journal /0 packet and supplied the R1 generation-seam analysis. Sol is therefore
disqualified as an independent implementer for PJ/0-portable and as a stranger for IH0's
station proper — permanently, and regardless of how the P5 result reads. This is not a
demerit; it is the price of having been useful.

**4.6 Independence is spent, never restored.** Once a mind has seen the canonical
internals or advised the construction, no subsequent forgetting, session reset, context
clearing, or substrate swap restores its eligibility. Statelessness cannot be verified
from outside; the provenance record can.

## Article 5 — Public versus hidden evidence

**5.1** The vector set has two halves. The **public** half is published with the
specification and may be used freely by any implementer. The **hidden** half is sealed
before the freeze, disclosed to no implementer, and is the half that carries the
conformance verdict's weight.

**5.2** Results are always reported **split**: public-vector agreement and hidden-vector
agreement, separately, never rolled together. A high public score with a poor hidden score
is a *transmission* result about the published materials, not a conformance result about
the implementation.

**5.3** Any hidden vector that leaks — by publication, by inclusion in a packet, by
quotation in a review, by an agent's summary — is **retired**, and its retirement is
recorded. It is not re-sealed and it is not reused.

**5.4** No document written under this charter may contain hidden-vector bodies, hidden
expected outputs, or algorithmic descriptions of the canonical evaluator's internals
beyond what the public specification states. A charter is public law; it is not a hint
sheet.

## Article 6 — First-run versus repaired conformance

**6.1** First-attempt results and post-repair results are **always reported separately**,
with the repair rounds counted. "Green after three repair rounds" and "green on first
execution" are different findings and may never be compressed into "green".

**6.2** A first-run transcript is preserved **unaltered**, with its exit code captured
directly. A transcript that was regenerated after a repair is labeled as such.

**6.3** The licensed sentence for a repaired conformance names the repair:

> Agreement was reached after N repair rounds; the first-attempt result was [stated
> exactly].

**6.4** Repairs to the *implementation under test* are permitted within a campaign if the
campaign's rules allow them and each is logged. Repairs to the **frozen seed** are not
(Article 3.2). Repairs to the **canonical evaluator** are governed by Article 9.

## Article 7 — Specification ambiguities and errata

**7.1 Underdetermination is a specification defect, not an implementer error.** When two
implementations diverge and the specification text does not settle which is correct, the
divergence is **underdetermined**.

**7.2 The mandatory response.** An underdetermination yields, in this order: (a) an
**erratum** to the specification, published; (b) a **new frozen seed** incorporating it;
(c) a **new campaign** run against that seed.

**7.3 The prohibition that gives 7.2 its teeth.** The second implementation is **never
tutored into imitation.** It is not told what the canonical implementation does; it is not
shown the canonical behavior and asked to match; the erratum states what the *law* is, not
what the incumbent *did*. An implementation corrected by being shown its target has
demonstrated obedience, not conformance, and its subsequent agreement carries no
evidential weight.

**7.4 The old campaign does not promote.** A campaign that surfaced an underdetermination
ends where it ended. Its outcome is reported with the underdetermination named. It is not
retroactively graded green by the erratum that followed it.

**7.5 Errata are cumulative and public.** The erratum record is part of the specification's
governed surface and is cited by every subsequent conformance claim.

## Article 8 — Disease-corpus conservation

**8.1 The disease corpus is the conserved quantity.** Every impossibility the project
claims exists twice: as law-prose, and as a planted executable disease whose witness must
turn **red**.

**8.2 The conservation law.**

> An extension is lawful only if every prior disease still turns its witness red,
> unmodified. New vocabulary and composition may be added; no old impossibility may be
> re-said. Growth is measured in what still cannot be uttered.

**8.3 Inheritance is total and unamendable.** Each generation inherits the full ancestral
disease corpus as a floor. Witnesses are not rewritten, retuned, relaxed, retired,
re-scoped, or marked expected-to-change. A witness whose red depends on a detail that an
extension legitimately alters is **re-expressed to preserve the same impossibility**, and
the re-expression is itself reviewed and recorded — it is never simply dropped.

**8.4 The detection rule.** A door reopened is a disease whose witness went quiet. Silence
is the alarm: a disease that no longer fires is treated as a **reopened impossibility**
until proven to be a harness fault, never the other way around.

**8.5 Teeth-check the conservation itself.** A conservation suite that has never been seen
to fail is untested, not passing. Each campaign plants at least one deliberate violation
and exhibits the corpus catching it before any clean run is credited.

## Article 9 — Evaluator-modification prohibitions

**9.1** Under every station above L0, the canonical evaluator, validator, grammar, and
fixtures are **unmodified** for the duration of the campaign. "Without evaluator
modification" is a load-bearing clause of every licensed sentence, and it means byte-level
unchanged, evidenced by hash, not asserted.

**9.2** A campaign in which the evaluator was modified — for any reason, including to fix
a genuine defect the campaign exposed — **yields no station claim**. The modification is a
finding; the campaign is reported and closed; a new freeze opens a new campaign.

**9.3** No special-casing, ever: dispatch on program name, hash, author, fixture identity,
or campaign label is a disqualifying defect wherever it is found, and the absence of such
dispatch is demonstrated mechanically, not asserted in prose.

**9.4** Fixture doors, escape hatches, and "just for this run" flags are evaluator
modifications by another name and fall under 9.2.

## Article 10 — Claim language after green, red, or ambiguous outcomes

**10.1 After GREEN.** Only the station's licensed sentence, verbatim, with its qualifiers
attached. The qualifiers travel with the claim into every downstream document —
abstracts, front doors, commit messages, and summaries included. A licensed sentence
stripped of its qualifiers is an unlicensed claim.

**10.2 After RED.** The red is **preserved, not repaired inside the campaign**, and it is
publishable. The licensed form names what failed and at what scope. A red result is never
described as "inconclusive", never quietly re-run until green, and never re-scoped after
the fact so that the failing case falls outside the claim.

**10.3 After AMBIGUOUS.** An outcome that neither discharges nor refutes is reported as
**not detected**, with the reason. Specifically: **a straddle-zero result is "not
detected", never "null confirmed"** (house rider). A null may be *confirmed* only under a
pre-declared equivalence margin δ, with the entire confidence interval inside [−δ, +δ],
and δ anchored to the question's effect scale rather than to the instrument's noise floor.

**10.4 Bounds are about magnitude, not existence.** Tightening an exclusion bound is a
statement about **magnitude-if-it-exists**, never evidence against existence. "We narrowed
the possible size of the effect" is licensed; "we weakened the case for the effect" is not.

**10.5 Counts.** Counts are stated as **"X executed + Y N/A"**, never rolled up into a
single total (house rider). "68 executed + 3 N/A" is the licensed form; "71 passed" is not.

**10.6 Hedging is not demotion.** Wrapping an over-large claim in "possibly", "arguably",
or "may suggest" while it keeps its position and its role demotes nothing. Demote by
**recategorizing** — a different station, a different section, an explicit smaller size —
never by softening adjectives.

**10.7 Verification is shown or declared compressed.** Any sentence containing "verified",
"traced", "checked", or "confirmed" either exhibits its load-bearing step or is written as
"traced, compressed", so the compression is visible and challengeable.

**10.8 A diagnosis is a claim.** An explanation of *why* something failed is subject to the
same evidentiary standard as any other claim. Either exhibit the evidence or write "cause
unverified". A wrong diagnosis closes an investigation; a missing one leaves it open.

## Article 11 — Owner jurisdiction over adoption

**11.1 Adoption is an owner act.** It is never inferred from green tests, from a clean
conformance run, from an outside reader's approval, from a sibling's agreement, from a
review's verdict, or from the passage of time.

**11.2 Station discharge is not adoption.** Discharging a station licenses a *sentence*.
It does not adopt an artifact, does not place anything on a governing floor, does not
authorize merge or publication, and does not create adoptability.

**11.3 Approving commentary is not adoption, and is not a witness.** An outside reader's
"crossed" verdict — including Sol's — is one reading. Where the reader was given the
project's own materials, it is a **shared-input** reading and measures the corpus's
attractor as much as the fact of the matter.

**11.4 Candidates only.** Everything under this charter is a candidate until the owner
adopts it, by name, in a record.

**11.5 The charter binds itself.** This charter does not become law by being read, agreed
with, cited, or acted upon. If the owner declines it, its station names carry no authority
and the documents drafted under it are drafts.

## Article 13 — Succession is not a station

**13.1** "Spiritual successor to Lisp" is **not on the ladder and is not discharged by any
station or combination of stations.** It is a title conferred by a tradition over time —
sustained lineage, use, extensibility, and influence — and no experiment in this lab can
grant it.

**13.2** The licensed treatment: *candidate, not earned title.* The project may say what it
inherits (programs as inspectable symbolic structures) and what it renounces (eval-trust,
unbounded extensibility, provisional retry, unrestricted semantic power). It may not say
what it has become.

**13.3** The charter's title contains the word "succession" because it governs the
**claims made along the way**, not because succession is an outcome this ladder can
deliver.
