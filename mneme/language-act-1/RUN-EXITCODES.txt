      exit 4    the run VOIDS: it names the variable and its value, creates NO
                journal store, runs no arm, and does NOT report a pass.
      This is exercised as the controls' first plant.  It caught a real defect
      during the build: the selftest originally called the pre-flight in
      advisory mode and went on to run arms with the death switch live, dying
      at exit 7 inside the frontier window.  A gate that reports and continues
      is not a gate.

         (exit 1 is the INTENDED result of the host-fault proof against the
          unrepaired handler — the RED capture; see §11 of the RETURN)
      3  refused to write a run root inside the subject tree
      4  VOID (a process-ending switch was set; not a pass, not a failure)
      7  capability /2's planted in-window death (a stage child only)

READING THE COUNTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Counts are DERIVED by the harnesses from the verdicts actually rendered; none
is hard-coded, and the specimen's total is the orchestrator's own count plus
the renumbered CHECK lines its stage children emitted.  A stage child that dies
without its RESULT sentinel contributes its CHECK lines and no sentinel — which
is exactly what the truncation control proves is detectable.
