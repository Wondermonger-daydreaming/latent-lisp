## 1. The question, stated so it cannot inflate

Can Lisp+ become more expressive while keeping its impossibilities invariant?

OG0 asks whether **outsider authors can construct materially new applications through a
declared public extension mechanism, without evaluator modification, and without any
inherited disease witness going quiet.** It does not ask whether the language is
open-ended, general, useful, or better than alternatives. Those are other questions and
other stations (Charter §2.1).

**The licensed phrase, and the only one:**

> evidence of disease-conserving generativity across unseen application topologies

**The bound that makes the phrase honest.** Finite experiments cannot prove literal
open-endedness. Every OG0 result is evidence *across the topologies actually tested*, with
those topologies named. "Open-ended", "unbounded", "arbitrary domains", and
"general-purpose" are forbidden as descriptions of any result this campaign can produce
(Charter §1.OG.3).

## 2. Requirements on outsider-authored applications

Each candidate application must satisfy **all** of the following. Failure of any one makes
the specimen non-qualifying — which is itself a recordable finding, not a reason to relax
the requirement.

**R1 — Materially differently topologized, twice over.** The application's causal skeleton
must differ materially from the construction examples **and** from every other application
in the same campaign. Same-shape applications in different vocabulary are one specimen, not
several. Topological distinctness is declared in advance, in prose, per specimen, and is
adjudicated before the run — never argued for afterward from a green result.

*Taken skeletons at the time of drafting (labels only, so distinctness is satisfiable):*
gated publication · duty-chain with two stopping modes · itinerary with
refusal-as-information · harvest-aggregation with quality gate · clearance-gate with
sterilization contingency. An OG0 specimen must be a sixth shape or later.

**R2 — Written from public materials.** Author works from the published specification, the
public authoring materials, and the declared extension mechanism's documentation. No
implementation source, no repair witnesses, no execution transcripts, no prior holdout
programs, no hidden vectors. Exposure statement filed **before** authoring; disclosed
exposure is a qualifier on the claim, undisclosed exposure discovered afterward voids the
specimen (Charter §4.4).

**R3 — New domain content arrives through a declared public extension mechanism.** The
mechanism must exist, be published, and be documented *before* the campaign's freeze. New
domain content that arrives any other way — a private fixture addition, a one-off flag, a
chair-supplied bridge — is an evaluator modification under another name and disqualifies
the specimen (Charter §9.4).

**R4 — Runs without evaluator modification.** Canonical evaluator, validator, grammar, and
governing fixtures byte-unchanged for the whole campaign, evidenced by hash. A specimen
that required a change is a **finding**, preserved; the change is not made inside the
campaign (Charter §9.1–9.2).

**R5 — Preserves all inherited disease witnesses.** Every disease in the inherited corpus
still turns its witness **red, unmodified**, with the extension mechanism in play and with
each specimen loaded. A witness that goes quiet is treated as a **reopened impossibility**
until proven a harness fault — never the reverse (Charter §8.4).

**R6 — Produces matching observations under J1 and J2.** Each specimen is run under the
canonical judge (J1) and the independently seeded second judge (J2), and the observations
must match. This is why OG0 follows portable conformance: without J2 there is one reading,
not two. Divergences are adjudicated to specification text, never to either
implementation's behavior, and each adjudication that requires an erratum ends the campaign
into a new seed (Charter §7).

**R7 — Correctly refuses invalid authority and invalid evidence.** Each specimen must
exercise, and be witnessed exercising, at least one refusal on invalid authority and one on
invalid evidence — refused **before** any consequential act, with the refusal legible and
typed. A specimen that only ever succeeds tests expressivity and not conservation.

**R8 — Supports local repair without constitutional surgery.** When a specimen is refused
at validation, the author must be able to repair it by changing the program locally, using
the refusal diagnostic alone, without amending the specification, the grammar, the
extension mechanism, or the evaluator. Repair rounds are **counted and reported
separately** from the first attempt (Charter §6.1). "Repairable only by changing the law"
is a red result about the language, and it is publishable as one.

## 3. What the campaign records

Per specimen: declared topology and its adjudication; exposure statement; first-attempt
outcome with transcript preserved unaltered and exit code captured directly; repair round
count and each round's diagnostic; J1/J2 observation comparison with divergence count;
disease-corpus status (every witness, red/quiet, unmodified/re-expressed); refusal
witnesses for R7; evaluator hash before and after.

Per campaign: the frozen seed (commit + tree), the specimen roster with declared
distinctness, the public/hidden evidence split reported separately (Charter §5.2), the
conservation suite's own teeth-check (at least one deliberate violation planted and
exhibited caught, before any clean run is credited — Charter §8.5), and every
non-qualifying specimen with the requirement it failed.

**Nothing rolls up.** Counts are "X executed + Y N/A". First-run and repaired results stay
separate. Public and hidden stay separate.

## 5. P5's standing in this campaign — fixed now, so it cannot drift later

**P5 is recorded as the first bounded inhabitation specimen. It is not retroactively
relabelled as proof of open-endedness, and it is not an OG0 specimen.**

P5 is a *fifth causal skeleton* authored through the frozen surface; OG0 requires a
*declared public extension mechanism* carrying new domain content, which P5 did not use and
which did not exist at P5's time. P5 predates this protocol and satisfies neither R2 (its
author's exposure is disclosed and substantial) nor R3.

Its governing claim, quoted whole, remains the only form in which it may be cited here:

> A post-freeze holdout program authored on a different model substrate by a
> non-implementation author, with disclosed prior source/transcript exposure and
> advisory participation, was accepted through the frozen public candidate surface and
> behaved exactly as predicted, without evaluator modification.

When an OG0 campaign reports its specimen count, **P5 is not among them.**

## 6. What this campaign cannot resolve, whatever it returns

It cannot establish open-endedness (§1). It cannot establish that models author better in
Lisp+ than in anything else — that is LM0, and green OG0 evidence supplies no part of it
(Charter §2.1). It cannot confer succession (Charter §13). It cannot adopt anything: green
specimens license a phrase, and adoption remains an owner act (Charter §11).

And it cannot make the language larger by proving it safe. Any language stays safe by
saying almost nothing; any language becomes expressive by quietly reopening every forbidden
door. OG0 is the attempt to have the first without the second, and its only real instrument
is the corpus of things that still cannot be said.
