4.  JUDGMENT CALLS FLAGGED FOR THE CHAIR, NOT DECIDED HERE
--------------------------------------------------------------------

J-1.  DOES AUTHOR-GUIDE.md + GRAMMAR + CONTRACT SUFFICE FOR AN IMPLEMENTER?
      Assessment: it is the best surface the lane has, and it is still not
      sufficient.  AUTHOR-GUIDE.md is the strongest document in the packet —
      it is the only one that states the observable boundary in full (§4
      act-summary readers, §6 the matching law and the closed axes, §7 the
      refusal/error boundary, §8 the environment declarations, §10 the caps).
      Its opening claim -- "This guide plus the package's exports is the whole
      surface" -- is close to true FOR AN AUTHOR.  For an IMPLEMENTER it is
      not, because "the package's exports" is a Common Lisp package, and the
      guide never states:
        (a) the identifier CASE rule.  §8 says slot and input names are
            "matched case-insensitively".  The implementation matches via
            (symbol-name ident) after the CL reader has up-cased the text; no
            explicit case-folding exists anywhere
            (grep for string-equal/char-equal/equalp in ma0-eval.lisp,
             ma0-environment.lisp, ma0-structures.lisp -> no hits).
            The guide describes a READTABLE ARTIFACT as if it were a law.
            J2 CANNOT IMPLEMENT THIS CORRECTLY FROM THE PACKET.
        (b) the act-identity digest algorithm.  §4 says "the 64-character
            act-identity digest segment" — length only.
        (c) the store-id encoding (the suite asserts store-id-length=74).
        (d) the classify-act-frames vocabulary (:ACT-REPORTED,
            :BINDING-DECLARED-UNPAIRED appear in the private oracle and in no
            public doc).
        (e) any normative serialization of a program result.  Today the
            observable is CL printed output.
      => RECOMMENDATION (chair's call): the packet is INSUFFICIENT until
         NORMATIVE-OBSERVATION-FORMAT-0 supplies (e) and adjudicates (a).
         Items (b)(c)(d) may instead be declared OUT OF SCOPE for J2 — which
         is a real and defensible option, and would shrink the portable bank.
