## One Act tree-membership ruling

Hash-by-tree-membership satisfies the **cryptographic inclusion** part of “cites or hashes them,” but does not alone establish **normative incorporation**.

A Git tree hash proves that particular bytes occupied particular paths in the adopted candidate tree. It does not, by itself, prove that the owner intended every blob in that tree—including material under `_staging/`—to become governing One Act law. Git is a superb notary of luggage; it is less gifted at determining which suitcase contained the constitution.

Round OA must therefore decide two separate propositions:

### OA-I — Cryptographic inclusion

For each of the five documents, prove:

* exact blob identity;
* exact path;
* membership in the candidate tree;
* containment in the adoption-time bundle;
* byte identity with the present tracked copy;
* absence of post-adoption drift.

Tree membership is sufficient evidence for this proposition.

### OA-N — Normative incorporation

For each document and, where necessary, each clause, determine whether the owner’s adoption act actually incorporated it as One Act /0 law.

Relevant evidence includes:

* the adoption record’s exact scope language;
* whether the record adopts the whole candidate tree, a named lane, a named packet, or a narrower enumerated set;
* whether the closure transcript identifies the five-document normative set;
* whether owner rulings during R1/R2/R2.1 explicitly adjudicate clauses in those documents;
* whether adopted source and gates cite those clauses as governing law;
* whether any record excludes `_staging`, candidate documents, or non-lane paths from normative adoption;
* whether the documents were merely carried in the tree as construction material.

Category 1 requires both OA-I and OA-N.

If cryptographic inclusion is proven but normative incorporation remains unproven, classify the material as category 2—reviewed candidate material—not category 1.

Source citations to `contract §N` are strong evidence that the contract was operative during construction, but they do not substitute for owner scope. Record both forms of evidence and let the recovery return expose any tension.
