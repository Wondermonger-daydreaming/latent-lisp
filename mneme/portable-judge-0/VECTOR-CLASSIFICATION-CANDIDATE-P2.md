## 3. **[P1] THE LAYER TAGGING**

### 3.0 Definitions, and the discipline

Ruling 3 enumerates **eleven language-layer behaviors** J2 implements: *datum ingestion ·
validation · bindings and scope · matching · branch selection · terminal discipline · result
construction · summary ordering · copy/ownership behavior at the observable boundary ·
refusal behavior · determinism.* The tag is defined against that list and the Act Oracle
envelope (`NORMATIVE-OBSERVATION-FORMAT-0-CANDIDATE-P1.md` §9):

| Tag | Definition |
|---|---|
| **LANG** | The vector's expectation is fully determined by the eleven behaviors **given the frozen case data** (source octets + declared environment + Act Oracle envelope). A clean-room J2 can produce the expected value from the packet and the envelope. |
| **SUBSTRATE-DEP** | The expectation's **value is produced by adopted-stack behavior** — One Act /0 arm execution, journal frames and prefix lengths, act-identity digests, store-ids, Capability recognition, Surface /2 derivation, agreement/classification vocabularies — reachable at L/0 only through the Act Oracle. J2 either transcribes it from the envelope (a transcription check, not a semantics check) or cannot produce it at all. |
| **MIXED** | One assertion carries both facets. The facet split is stated for every MIXED row; a MIXED vector is a **candidate for splitting into two vectors**, and saying so is the tag's main practical use. |

**Two disciplines the tag is held to.**

1. **The tag is about LAYER, not about CONTAMINATION.** They are orthogonal and the frozen
   original's §3 (contamination) is carried forward unchanged in §6. A vector can be LANG and
   contaminated (`W-V-DATA`: pure validator law, expressed in CL read syntax). A vector can be
   SUBSTRATE-DEP and uncontaminated (a verdict string transcribed from the envelope is
   perfectly portable and tells you nothing).
2. **The oracle being an input does not make its outputs LANG.** If it did, every vector would
   be LANG and the tag would be free. The test is: *does the judge **compute** this, or
   **carry** it?*

**Everything below was assigned by static reading. Nothing was executed.** The reading for the
90 scenario checks was `sed -n '834,975p' ma0-selftest-suite.lisp` — the eleven expectation
lists in full, verbatim — and every expectation string was tagged individually, which is why
§3.3 is printed at expectation-string granularity rather than summarized.
