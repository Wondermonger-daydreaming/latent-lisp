## 1. The two campaign targets

Ruling 3: **the two-layer decomposition is accepted; the original center claim is not.** The Act
Oracle Interface is a legitimate instrument for testing the portability of the Many Acts language
layer. **It does not constitute a second implementation of the complete Lisp+ judge or Mneme
runtime.** The campaign is therefore divided into two explicitly distinct targets. They are not
two phases of one claim and not a ladder; the smaller one does not imply the larger.

### 1.1 PortJ-L/0 — Language-layer portable conformance

**This is the target this protocol governs.**

**J2 implements** (the eleven items, exactly as ruled — this list is the work-list and the
boundary of the work-list):

1. datum ingestion;
2. validation;
3. bindings and scope;
4. matching;
5. branch selection;
6. terminal discipline;
7. result construction;
8. summary ordering;
9. copy/ownership behavior at the observable boundary;
10. refusal behavior;
11. determinism.

**J2 consumes a frozen Act Oracle transcript for substrate-layer operations.**

**The candidate hypothesis, verbatim as ruled, and framed as a hypothesis under test — never as
a finding:**

> Given only the frozen public normative packet and frozen Act Oracle Interface, a clean-room
> implementer can construct a non-Common-Lisp implementation of the Many Acts /0 language layer
> whose externally observable judgments conform to the canonical language-layer evaluator across
> the frozen normative cases.

**A green result licenses only, verbatim:**

> A clean-room non-Common-Lisp implementation of the Many Acts /0 language layer, consuming the
> frozen Act Oracle Interface, conformed to the canonical language-layer evaluator on the named
> frozen vector set.

**It must never be shortened to "an independent Lisp+ implementation exists."**

That prohibition is binding on every artifact, abstract, table header, commit message, slide,
and summary line of this campaign, at every citation, in perpetuity. The licensed sentence is
the *only* success sentence; where space forbids the whole sentence, the correct move is to cite
this section, never to compress the claim.

The hypothesis's three load-bearing phrases, and what each is made to mean mechanically:

| Phrase | Made mechanical by |
|---|---|
| *only the frozen public normative packet and frozen Act Oracle Interface* | §7 sealed packet + §8 clean-room provenance + the implementer's non-consultation attestation |
| *externally observable judgments* | §5 observable boundary, retargeted to the language layer, with its exclusions binding on the harness |
| *across the frozen normative cases* | §9 ordering: the J2 freeze declaration lands **before** the holdout is opened; and §10's requirement that the vector set be **named** in the licensed sentence |

**What a green campaign would NOT earn — drafted now so no one may inflate it later.** It would
not earn: that Lisp+ is portable in general; that the *act substrate* is portable (§3 — this is
the whole point of the split); that the specification is complete (a green run over cases we
thought to write is silent about cases we did not); that the base lane is adopted or adoptable;
that either implementation is *correct* against anything except the other and the written law;
that PortJ-F/0's question has been answered, partially answered, or made likely.

**The null is publishable and pre-committed as such.** A campaign that ends in SPECIFICATION
UNDERDETERMINATION (FAILURE-TAXONOMY-P1 class 2) — J1 and J2 disagreeing with both readings

reported at full strength. So is a REFERENCE DEFECT. Neither may be reported as a near-miss, and
neither may be repaired into a green by tutoring J2. The campaign's *existing* substantive
result is already of this shape and is restated unshortened, at the owner's own wording:

> The present public law is insufficient for a clean-room second implementation without
> invention, oracle mediation, or consultation of implementation source in at least twenty-eight
> registered places.

That is a finding about semantic extractability. It is not an embarrassment to be hurried past,
and it is **not** evidence for or against either target.

## 2. Governing principle (unchanged)

> **The substrate executes the judge; it is not itself the law.**

Agreement with Common Lisp's incidental object representations, control flow, condition system,
printer, or implementation quirks **is not conformance**. Conformance is agreement with the
*specification-owned meaning* of Lisp+ forms.

Four operational consequences, each of which the harness enforces rather than requests:

1. **A test that can only be passed by a Common Lisp is not a conformance test.** It is an ORACLE
   CONTAMINATION finding against the test (taxonomy class 3), rewritten or voided, and never
   counted against J2.
2. **J1's behavior is evidence about the law only where the law says so.** Where J1 does a thing
   the written packet does not determine, J1 is *a* reading, not *the* reading. The older
   implementation does not win by seniority (taxonomy class 5).
3. **The comparator compares observation envelopes, never implementations.** It never reads J2's
   source to decide a verdict. Source is read only in classification (ADJUDICATION-P1), and only
   to distinguish a port defect from an underdetermination.
4. **The specification's silences are findings, not defaults.** Where the packet does not
   determine a required behavior, the correct output is a recorded **spec deficit**, never a quiet
   patch and never an oral answer. §13 points at the register that holds them.

## 3. Scoping: the two layers, and the Act Oracle Interface

### 3.1 Why the seam exists

The Many Acts /0 judge sits on top of an adopted Common Lisp substrate it does not own. Its `act`
step executes an adopted **One Act /0** arm through `run-act` plus the lane's public
re-composition `ma0-complete-act`, which in turn drives Journal /0 appends, Capability /0 + /1
authority, Capability /2 uncertain-effect arms, and Surface /2 derivation (base contract §4). Its
`derive` step folds a **Surface /2** seat-outcome over the store's validated prefix.

| Layer | Contents | In scope for PortJ-L/0? |
|---|---|---|
| **L — the language layer** | datum ingestion · the closed validator · binding classes and scope · the matching law and branch selection · terminal discipline · result and summary assembly · ownership/copy semantics · the error/refusal boundary · determinism and ordering | **YES — this is what PortJ-L/0 tests, and it is the eleven-item work-list of §1.1** |
| **S — the substrate layer** | One Act /0 arm execution · the journal store · Capability /0//1//2 · Surface /2 derivation · the act-identity digest · agreement and correspondence verdicts | **NO — oracle-mediated at L/0; it is PortJ-F/0's subject** |

### 3.2 What the Act Oracle Interface is

**The AOI is the declared seam.** For every canonical case, the harness supplies J2 (and, in
replay, J1) with a **canonical act transcript**: a data-only record giving, for each act
invocation in textual order, the arm, the resulting disposition, class, act-id-hex, and verdict;
and for each derive request, the seat resolution result and the three closed facets with their
standings; and, for each step boundary, the store's validated-prefix length (so
`derive`-appends-nothing is checkable without a store). The AOI is **data**, produced by J1 in an
instrumented native run, frozen with the case.

**The transcript is an INPUT that both judges share. It is never a compared observation.**
Anything the transcript supplies is, by construction, not a thing J2 computed, and the comparator
may not score it as agreement (ADJUDICATION-P1 §4). Presenting oracle-supplied values as
conformance is the single most available way to inflate this campaign's result.

### 3.3 What the AOI costs the claim, said plainly

Under this scoping the campaign tests whether the *judge* — validation, binding, selection,
terminals, observation, ownership, determinism — is portable. It does **not** test whether the
*act substrate* is portable, and a green campaign says nothing about that. **In the original
protocol this cost was disclosed in a paragraph attached to a larger claim; under Ruling 3 the
cost is instead built into the claim itself** — the licensed sentence names the oracle out loud
("consuming the frozen Act Oracle Interface"), so a reader cannot take the claim larger than the
campaign supports without contradicting its own text. The fork the original left open ("the owner
may prefer to amend the claim's wording") is **closed by Ruling 3, in favor of amending the
wording.**

### 3.4 The AOI replay adapter — form, gate, and VOID (Ruling 3, binding)

An oracle interface is a confound factory: if J1 behaves differently in replay than natively, the
AOI is measuring itself. The owner's condition governs its construction:

1. **The replay adapter should PREFERABLY remain an external harness or wrapper.** The first
   design attempt must be one that does not modify J1 at all — instrumentation and replay driven
   from outside the canonical implementation. A wrapper that never edits J1 has no identity
   question to answer, which is why it is preferred.
2. **If modifying J1 is unavoidable**, three conditions bind, all three:
   a. the adapter **becomes a separately identified pre-campaign candidate** — its own named
      artifact, its own owner disposition, adjudicable apart from the campaign; it is not a
      harness detail and may not be introduced inside a campaign parcel;
   b. it **preserves the original native path** — added as optional parameters defaulting to
      existing behavior, so every existing call site resolves exactly as before; no existing
      function renamed, no existing default changed;
   c. it **passes a byte-identity gate over the FULL INHERITED FLOOR before the campaign
      freezes** — not the campaign's own cases, not a sample: the entire inherited floor of the
      base lane and its predecessors, run before and after, with check-lines and values shown
      **identical as a diff in the record**, never asserted as a sentence.
3. **Any failure of that identity gate VOIDS the campaign before J2 comparison.** Not a caveat,
   not a note, not a footnote in a return: the campaign does not reach comparison. The correct
   move is to fix the adapter and re-gate.
4. **Native-vs-replay envelope identity** remains additionally required for every public-bank
   case (ADJUDICATION-P1 §3). It is a *narrower* check than 2(c) and does not substitute for it.

**Ordering note, load-bearing:** the identity gate is a **prerequisite**, evaluated **before the
campaign freezes** and therefore before any J2 comparison exists to be tempted by. A gate
evaluated after a comparison is a gate whose outcome someone already knows the cost of.

## 4. Substrate recommendation (unchanged)

**J2: Python.** Chosen for two reasons and no third: (a) **sufficient substrate distance** — a
different reader, different symbol/interning model, different equality lattice, different
condition/exception discipline, different copy semantics, different numeric tower behavior; these
are precisely the axes on which a Common-Lisp-shaped specification leaks; and (b) **low
implementation cost**, which keeps the campaign's cost inside one implementer's sitting and keeps
the finding about *the specification* rather than about the implementer's stamina.

Explicitly **not** chosen: Rust. Rust is **reserved for a possible J3** on a later campaign. This
campaign tests **semantic portability**, not the endurance of an implementer wrestling an
ownership type system in ceremonial combat; a red run in Rust would be ambiguous between "the spec
is Common-Lisp-shaped" and "the borrow checker ate the afternoon," and an ambiguous red is the one
outcome this design exists to prevent.

Substrate constraints on J2, minimal and normative:

- **Standard library only.** No parsing framework, no s-expression package, no Lisp interoperation
  library, no reader ported from Common Lisp. A J2 that imports someone else's Lisp reader has
  moved the question, not answered it.
- **Deterministic by construction.** No hash-order-dependent output, no clock, no PID, no
  filesystem enumeration order, no locale-dependent case operations reaching normative comparison,
  no randomness. `PYTHONHASHSEED` independence is a checked property (ADJUDICATION-P1 §6), not a
  habit.
- **No network at any point**, during construction or execution (§8).

## 5. The observable boundary — language layer

Everything in §5.1 is **normative and compared**. Everything in §5.2 is **excluded** and the
harness may not consult it. Everything in §5.3 is **oracle-supplied** and is neither.

The enumeration is grounded in the base lane's public law — GRAMMAR §§1–6, AUTHOR-GUIDE §§0–10,
CONTRACT §§2–8, FAILURE-MATRIX §§1–6. Where an item is determinable only from the canonical
implementation's source, it is marked **[DEFICIT]** and carried to §13 rather than silently
promoted to law. **The enumeration itself is unchanged from the frozen original**; what Round P
changed is the *retargeting* (§5.3 is now explicit, and each OB carries its layer tag) — no OB was
added, removed, widened, or narrowed.

### 5.1 Observable (normative, language-layer)

**OB-1 — Datum ingestion.** [L] A program file holds **exactly one** form; an empty file and a
file with a second form are both refused. Reader-evaluation is disabled at the reader boundary, so
a `#.` form dies below the validator. Observable: accept/refuse, and the refusal code. **[DEFICIT
— the canonical ingestion rule is Common Lisp's `read`.** The written law says "one form,"
"`*read-eval*` NIL," "read into the program namespace"; it does not specify, for a non-CL
substrate, the token grammar, case conversion of symbols, keyword syntax, integer syntax, string
escapes, comment syntax, or dotted-pair rejection. This is the single largest deficit in the packet
and the most likely site of a class-2 verdict. **Ruling 6 is the owner's directed candidate cure
and is NOT yet adopted, implemented, or frozen — see §14.**]

**OB-2 — Validation disposition.** [L] Accepted, or refused. On refusal the observation carries the
**condition family** (one of the exported condition types: `ma0-source-refused`,
`ma0-binding-refused`, `ma0-pattern-refused`, `ma0-environment-refused`,
`ma0-authority-slot-unfilled`, `ma0-environment-stale`, `ma0-composition-divergence`) and the
**refusal code** (`V-SHAPE`, `V-READ`, `V-DATA`, `V-PKG`, `V-BIND`, `V-FIELD`, `V-AUTH`,
`V-RES-AUTH`, `V-ARM`, `V-PATTERN`, `V-TERM`, `V-RETRY`, and the environment/runtime family).
**Refusal *message text* is NOT observable** — the base lane states in its own sources that
condition report text is not a stable interface, and the harness honors that. **[DEFICIT — no
complete code table is published, the codes' datatype is unstated, and the law→family→code mapping
exists only in source.]**

**OB-3 — Refusal footprint.** [L, oracle-accounted] A validation refusal has **zero footprint**: no
store, no journal append, no mint, no act. Observable as the store-prefix length and the
act-summary list being untouched (via the AOI's prefix accounting). This is FAILURE-MATRIX
W-V-FOOTPRINT. *What is under test is that J2 produces no act request at all — not the store's own
behavior, which is substrate.*

**OB-4 — The program result record.** [L] `program-name` (string) · `disposition` ∈
{`:completed`, `:refused`} · `value` (the terminal payload datum, **nesting preserved exactly**) ·
`refusal-code` (the program-authored KEYWORD from a `refuse` terminal) · `refusal-detail` (the
optional payload datum, **nesting preserved exactly** — a `(list …)` payload is a nested structure
and is compared as such) · `act-summaries` (ordered, **oldest act first**) · `store-id` (a **label
only**; R1-F5 records that it is content-derived and cannot discriminate two stores, so the harness
compares it as a field and never uses it as an identity).

**OB-5 — The act summary.** [L for existence/order/carriage; S for values] `arm` (string) ·
`act-id-hex` (the 64-character act-identity digest segment, or absent) · `disposition` ∈
{`:returned`, `:refused`, `:interrupted`, `:host-fault`, `:mint-refused`} · `class` ∈ {`:a`, `:b`,
`:c-i`, `:c-ii`, `:d`, `:unclassifiable`, `:unpaired-f1`} · `verdict` (agreement-verdict string, or
absent on a mint-refused act). **Under the AOI these values are supplied per case; what is under
test is which summaries exist, in what order, with which fields carried through** — not the digest
arithmetic, not the classification, not the verdict, all of which are substrate-layer and
oracle-supplied (§5.3).

**OB-6 — Branch selection and single-arm execution.** [L] Clauses tested in **textual order**; the
**first holding** clause selected; `otherwise` iff none holds; **exactly one arm's steps
evaluated**. Observable through the arm's consequences: the act summaries produced, the terminal
reached, and the untaken arm's **absence of any act summary and any store footprint**. (This is the
base lane's W-BRANCH-ONE. The commissioned phrase "prohibited-branch nonexecution" maps **here**,
and to nothing else — see §6.)

**OB-7 — The matching law.** [L] A value atom holds **iff** the facet's standing is `:present`
**and** the facet value is exactly the pattern value. An absence-keyword atom
(`:absent-from-evidence`, `:malformed-in-evidence`) holds iff the standing is exactly that keyword.
`:and` holds iff every atom holds. Over an act-result, the two axes are `:disposition` and
`:class`, and an absence-keyword atom **never holds**. **No truthiness participates anywhere**: a
non-nil facet holds nothing. Observable as selection outcome across the holdout's discrimination
cases.

**OB-8 — Derive ≠ perform.** [L, oracle-accounted] A `derive` step **appends nothing**: the store's
validated-prefix length is invariant across every derive (AOI-accounted). `(field X AXIS)` over a
derived outcome yields the facet **value** when the standing is `:present`, and the **standing
keyword** otherwise (the typed absence is carried, never filled in with nil).

**OB-9 — Authority decisions.** [L] At **each** act step the slot's occupancy is retrieved from the
environment **by name, explicitly**. An unfilled slot refuses (`ma0-authority-slot-unfilled`) and
**no act begins**. An arm the environment does not admit refuses (`ma0-environment-refused`). Slot
and input names match **case-insensitively** between source identifiers and environment plans.
There is no ambient fallback: no dynamic variable, no default, no inference. **[DEFICIT — the exact
case-normalization rule (upcase of the string designator) is source-only, and its behavior on
non-ASCII is unstated. Ruling 6 is the owner's directed candidate cure; §14.]**

**OB-10 — Static arm-once discipline.** [L] Each of the seven arms may appear **at most once** in a
program text, refused **statically** (`V-ARM`) so the failure has no footprint.

**OB-11 — Terminal discipline and the error/refusal boundary.** [L] Every complete evaluation ends
in exactly one terminal; the last step of `:steps` and of every branch arm is a terminal (or a
branch all of whose arms are); steps after a terminal are refused as unreachable. A `refuse`
terminal is a **lawful outcome**: disposition `:refused`, orderly exit, runner exit code 0. An
**error is not an outcome**: a validator refusal, an environment refusal, an unbranched lane
condition, or a host fault **propagates** and is **never** converted into `:completed`; the
runner's exit code is nonzero. Observable: `(disposition | propagated-condition-family + code)` and
the **process exit code**.

**OB-12 — Ownership and immutability.** [L] Every public reader that hands back a string, a list,
or a payload hands back a **fresh deep copy including mutable leaves**. Mutating what a reader
returned changes **nothing** in the validated program, the environment, or the result. Observable
by a mutate-then-reread probe at every public reader; on a substrate whose strings are immutable
(Python), the observable is the *reread invariance*, which such a substrate satisfies structurally
— and the harness records that it was satisfied structurally rather than by a copy, because *how*
it is satisfied is not observable and must not be scored. (This is the ruling's item 9,
*"copy/ownership behavior at the observable boundary"* — the qualifier is the whole content.)

**OB-13 — Determinism and replay.** [L] The same source + the same declared environment + the same
frozen act transcript ⇒ a **byte-identical observation envelope** across repeated runs (≥2, in
separate processes, with hash-seed variation on J2). Output ordering is stable.

**OB-14 — Ordering.** [L] Steps evaluate in strict textual order, exactly once; `act` is the only
consequential step; act summaries are reported **oldest first**.

**OB-15 — Declared bounds and termination.** [L] Source depth and node count are **finite and
declared**; exceeding them produces a **deterministic typed refusal**, never a crash, a hang, or a
stack exhaustion. A **circular source refuses inside the bound** (R1/D3), and so does an oversize
datum met during ownership. **[DEFICIT — the bound *values* (depth 32, nodes 4096, ownership budget
65536) live only in the implementation's constants; public law says "declared constants" without
declaring them, so no clean-room implementer can conform on a boundary case.]**

### 5.2 Excluded (non-normative; the harness may not consult these)

Internal evaluation traces and intermediate data structures · host exception types, messages,
tracebacks, and the CL condition system's own machinery · condition **report text** · symbol
identity, interning, package objects, and `eq`-ness · object memory layout, struct printing, and
printed representations (including the environment's `print-unreadable-object` form — R1/D5 hid an
interior; it did not publish one) · function, method, module, class, and file names · control
structures, recursion vs iteration, dispatch style · the private environment **generation counter**
(package-internal, never exported: J2 need not have one — only the observable staleness *refusal*
is normative) · `store-id` used as an identity discriminator (R1-F5) · timing, memory, allocation
counts, process ids, temp-directory paths · the on-disk layout of J2's own implementation.

**Unless the specification explicitly makes one of these observable.** It presently does not. If a
future erratum does, it moves to §5.1 by amendment, never by a harness author's judgment
mid-campaign.

### 5.3 Oracle-supplied (neither observable nor excluded — an INPUT)

New section in this revision, made necessary by the L/0 retarget. These values reach the envelope
but are **not** things J2 computed, and the comparator **must not** count their agreement as
conformance (ADJUDICATION-P1 §4):

- the arm's resulting **disposition**, **class**, **act-id-hex**, and **verdict** for each act;
- the **seat resolution** result and the three closed facets with their **standings** for each
  derive;
- the store's **validated-prefix length** at each step boundary.

**What remains J2's, and therefore under test, at every one of these points:** *which* act
requests are issued, *in what order*, *whether one is issued at all*, and *how the returned values
are carried into the result record and the summaries*. The transcript answers "what did the act
do"; the campaign asks "did the judge ask, in the right order, and carry the answer correctly."

**Tampering with the transcript is a harness-detectable event, not a modeling assumption** — the
harness carries a teeth-check for it (ADJUDICATION-P1 §7), and a divergence caused by the
transcript adjudicates against the transcript, never against J2 (FAILURE-TAXONOMY-P1 §1.3a).

## 8. Clean-room provenance requirements (unchanged)

carried. The evidence is an attestation plus a structurally constrained environment.

**P-1 — Eligibility.** The J2 implementer is a **fresh human or a fresh model session** with no

frozen original**; every construction-loop participant; every reviewer who has read the canonical
implementation; **and any session with read access to this repository or its public mirror.**

**P-2 — The tree is a boot document.** A sibling or agent whose working directory is the lab repo
has already read the implementation, whether or not it opened the file — the lab's own standing
rule (`CLAUDE.md` §I-f: *"you cannot blind-test a sibling that shares your filesystem"*). J2 is
therefore constructed **outside** the repository, from the archive alone, in a directory
containing nothing else.

**P-3 — No network during construction or execution.** The public mirror is on the open internet
and contains the CL implementation; a search away is not clean-room. If network isolation cannot
be enforced, the implementer attests non-consultation **by name of the resource** and the
adjudicator records the weaker evidential standing explicitly, in the return, at every citation.

**P-4 — No oral tradition.** No question about the meaning of the specification is answered by
anyone. Ambiguities are **recorded as candidate deficits** in the implementer's own register and
delivered with the implementation. A construction-loop participant who answers such a question
converts the campaign's verdict from *conformance* to *tutoring*, and the taxonomy treats it as
class 2 with the answer preserved verbatim as the evidence.

**P-5 — Attestation, signed at delivery.** The implementer states: what was consulted (the packet,
by manifest hash); what was not (the enumerated withheld set, by name); whether the network was
reachable and whether it was used; who, if anyone, was consulted and about what; and their own
eligibility under P-1. An attestation is testimony and is recorded as such — **never as
verification**.

**P-6 — Same-root honesty, if J2 is a model.** A Claude session constructing J2 is same-root with
the chair; a green campaign then measures the *corpus attractor* as well as the specification.
This does not disqualify the run, and it **does** cap the claim: a same-root J2 earns
*"conformance under same-root provenance"*, and only a different family or a human earns the
unqualified word. The choice is the owner's; the caveat travels with the result either way.
