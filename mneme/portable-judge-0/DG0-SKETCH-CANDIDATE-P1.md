## 0. What the rename and the author-agnostic rewrite did *(Ruling 8)*

The frozen original named this campaign **OG0** and wrote the requirement of **outsider
authorship into its own definition**. Ruling 8 rules that "disease-conserving generativity
should be author-agnostic" and that "outsider authorship and generativity may later be
conjoined, but neither should be smuggled into the definition of the other."

Three consequences, each executed below:

1. **The node is renamed DG0 — Disease-conserving generativity /0.**
2. **The authorship constraint is REMOVED from the requirements.** What was requirement R2
   ("written from public materials" by an outsider) is replaced by an author-agnostic
   **disclosure** rule (§2, R2). Authorship is *recorded*, never *required*.
3. **The outsider requirement now lives in the composite**, not here: **COMP-OEI = CI0 ∧
   DG0** — "open-ended inhabitation" is a conjunction requiring both clean-room outsider
   authorship (**CI0**) and disease-conserving generativity across materially different
   topologies (**DG0**), and is **never earned by either conjunct alone** (Charter-P1
   §1.COMP.2).

The mechanism constraint is *not* an authorship constraint and stays: new domain content
must still arrive through a **declared public extension mechanism** (R3).

## 1. The question, stated so it cannot inflate

Can Lisp+ become more expressive while keeping its impossibilities invariant?

DG0 asks whether **authors — of any provenance — can construct materially new applications
through a declared public extension mechanism, without evaluator modification, and without
any inherited disease witness going quiet.** It does not ask whether the language is
open-ended, general, useful, better than alternatives, or inhabitable by strangers. Those
are other questions and other nodes (Charter-P1 §2.1).

**The licensed phrase, and the only one (Ruling 8):**

> evidence of disease-conserving generativity across the tested unseen application
> topologies

The word **"tested"** is part of the phrase. The topologies are **named and counted** in
every citation.

**The bound that makes the phrase honest.** Finite experiments cannot prove literal
open-endedness. Every DG0 result is evidence *across the topologies actually tested*.
**"Open-ended", "unbounded", "arbitrary domains", and "general-purpose" are forbidden** as
descriptions of any result this campaign can produce — and remain forbidden without the
composite qualification even where COMP-OEI is in play (Charter-P1 §1.DG.3, §1.COMP.4).

## 2. Requirements on candidate applications *(author-agnostic)*

Each candidate application must satisfy **all** of the following. Failure of any one makes
the specimen non-qualifying — which is itself a recordable finding, not a reason to relax
the requirement.

**R1 — Materially differently topologized, twice over.** The application's causal skeleton
must differ materially from the construction examples **and** from every other application
in the same campaign. Same-shape applications in different vocabulary are one specimen, not
several. Topological distinctness is declared in advance, in prose, per specimen, and is
adjudicated before the run — never argued for afterward from a green result.

*Taken skeletons at the time of drafting (labels only, so distinctness is satisfiable):*
gated publication · duty-chain with two stopping modes · itinerary with
refusal-as-information · harvest-aggregation with quality gate · clearance-gate with
sterilization contingency. A DG0 specimen must be a sixth shape or later.

**R2 — Exposure declared and recorded; authorship NOT constrained.** *(This slot formerly
held the outsider-authorship requirement; per Ruling 8 that requirement has moved to the
composite and is no longer part of DG0's definition.)*

- A DG0 specimen may be authored by **anyone** — a construction-loop participant, an
  advisor, a non-implementation author on another substrate, or a clean-room outsider.
- An **exposure statement is filed before authoring** and is recorded as a qualifier on the
  specimen record (Charter-P1 §4.4). Undisclosed exposure discovered afterward **voids the
  specimen**; disclosed exposure **does not disqualify it**.
- The precise and limited consequence of non-outsider authorship: such a specimen supplies
  **no CI0 evidence** and **cannot contribute its author to COMP-OEI**. It remains a fully
  qualifying DG0 specimen.
- Materials used are still recorded per specimen (which published documents, which
  extension-mechanism documentation, whether implementation source was consulted). Material
  provenance is **data on the record**, not a gate — except where R3 or R4 is thereby
  violated.

**R3 — New domain content arrives through a declared public extension mechanism.** The
mechanism must exist, be published, and be documented *before* the campaign's freeze. New
domain content that arrives any other way — a private fixture addition, a one-off flag, a
chair-supplied bridge — is an evaluator modification under another name and disqualifies
the specimen (Charter-P1 §9.4). **This is a mechanism constraint and survives the
author-agnostic rewrite unchanged.**

**R4 — Runs without evaluator modification.** Canonical evaluator, validator, grammar, and
governing fixtures byte-unchanged for the whole campaign, evidenced by hash. A specimen
that required a change is a **finding**, preserved; the change is not made inside the
campaign (Charter-P1 §9.1–9.2).

**R5 — Preserves all inherited disease witnesses.** Every disease in the inherited corpus
still turns its witness **red, unmodified**, with the extension mechanism in play and with
each specimen loaded. A witness that goes quiet is treated as a **reopened impossibility**
until proven a harness fault — never the reverse (Charter-P1 §8.4).

**R6 — Produces matching observations under J1 and J2.** Each specimen is run under the
canonical judge (J1) and a second judge (J2), and the observations must match. Divergences
are adjudicated to specification text, never to either implementation's behavior, and each
adjudication that requires an erratum ends the campaign into a new seed (Charter-P1 §7).

> **⚠ R6 IS UNDERDETERMINED BY THE RULINGS — registered as Charter-P1 tension T9, not
> resolved here.** With the portability campaign split into **PortJ-L/0** (a language-layer
> J2 consuming a frozen Act Oracle transcript) and **PortJ-F/0** (not yet openable, and
> blocked until the predecessor stack's normative law is publicly sufficient), it is not
> settled whether an oracle-mediated language-layer J2 can supply the DG0 comparison —
> especially for specimens whose new domain content arrives through an extension mechanism
> the frozen oracle transcript may not cover. Three readings are open and this sketch picks
> none: (a) a PortJ-L/0 J2 suffices, within the language layer only; (b) DG0 is blocked
> behind PortJ-F/0; (c) R6 is restated for author-agnostic specimens. **Owner ruling
> needed before any DG0 campaign opens.**

**R7 — Correctly refuses invalid authority and invalid evidence.** Each specimen must
exercise, and be witnessed exercising, at least one refusal on invalid authority and one on
invalid evidence — refused **before** any consequential act, with the refusal legible and
typed. A specimen that only ever succeeds tests expressivity and not conservation.

**R8 — Supports local repair without constitutional surgery.** When a specimen is refused
at validation, the author must be able to repair it by changing the program locally, using
the refusal diagnostic alone, without amending the specification, the grammar, the
extension mechanism, or the evaluator. Repair rounds are **counted and reported separately**
from the first attempt (Charter-P1 §6.1). "Repairable only by changing the law" is a red
result about the language, and it is publishable as one.

## 3. What the campaign records

Per specimen: declared topology and its adjudication; **exposure statement and authorship
provenance (recorded, not gating)**; whether the specimen is composite-eligible (i.e.
whether its author independently satisfies CI0's discharge condition — recorded as a
separate field so the conjunction can never be assembled after the fact); first-attempt
outcome with transcript preserved unaltered and exit code captured directly; repair round
count and each round's diagnostic; J1/J2 observation comparison with divergence count and
**which portable target supplied J2**; disease-corpus status (every witness, red/quiet,
unmodified/re-expressed); refusal witnesses for R7; evaluator hash before and after.

Per campaign: the frozen seed (commit + tree), the specimen roster with declared
distinctness, the public/hidden evidence split reported separately (Charter-P1 §5.2), the
conservation suite's own teeth-check (at least one deliberate violation planted and
exhibited caught, before any clean run is credited — Charter-P1 §8.5), and every
non-qualifying specimen with the requirement it failed.

**Nothing rolls up.** Counts are "X executed + Y N/A". First-run and repaired results stay
separate. Public and hidden stay separate. **Composite eligibility is recorded per
specimen and is never inferred from a campaign-level average.**

## 5. P5's standing in this campaign — fixed now, so it cannot drift later

**P5 is recorded as the project's first bounded inhabitation specimen in its specimen
history. That is a specimen-history label, not a node discharge: it is not open-endedness,
it is not CI0, and it is not a DG0 specimen.**

Under the Charter-P1 lattice, P5 is filed as evidence record **R-P5 — qualified
cross-substrate semantic-agreement evidence** (Charter-P1 §1.E.P5), which creates **no
edges** to any node.

**Why P5 is not a DG0 specimen, restated for the author-agnostic protocol.** In the frozen
original, P5 failed both R2 (its author's exposure is disclosed and substantial) and R3.
**The R2 failure no longer applies** — DG0 no longer constrains authorship, and disclosed
exposure is a recorded qualifier rather than a disqualification. **The R3 failure stands
and is sufficient:** DG0 requires new domain content to arrive through a *declared public
extension mechanism*, which P5 did not use and which did not exist at P5's time. P5 is a
*fifth causal skeleton* authored through the frozen surface; it predates this protocol.

Its governing claim, quoted whole, remains the only form in which it may be cited here:

> A post-freeze holdout program authored on a different model substrate by a
> non-implementation author, with disclosed prior source/transcript exposure and
> advisory participation, was accepted through the frozen public candidate surface and
> behaved exactly as predicted, without evaluator modification.

The exposure qualifier travels with it into every summary (Charter-P1 §1.E.P5.2, rider 7).

When a DG0 campaign reports its specimen count, **P5 is not among them.**

## 6. What this campaign cannot resolve, whatever it returns

Stated as a list because each item is a drift someone will otherwise try:

- **It cannot establish open-endedness** (§1). Finite runs license the tested-topologies
  phrase and nothing wider.
- **It cannot establish outsider capability.** DG0 is author-agnostic *by construction*, so
  a green DG0 says **nothing whatever** about whether a mind outside the construction and
  adjudication orbit can inhabit the language. That is **CI0**, and the only route from DG0
  to an inhabitation-flavoured sentence is the composite, which requires CI0 to be
  discharged on its own evidence (Charter-P1 §1.COMP.2).
- **It cannot establish model benefit.** Whether models author better in Lisp+ than in
  unrestricted Common Lisp, tagged JSON, a typed DSL, or structured natural-language plans
  is **LM0**, which has no incoming edges; green DG0 evidence supplies no part of it
  (Charter-P1 §1.LM.5, §2.1).
- **It cannot establish portable conformance**, at either target. Running specimens under a
  J2 uses portability; it does not demonstrate it.
- **It cannot establish usability**, adoption-readiness, or that anyone should write in
  this language.
- **It cannot confer succession** (Charter-P1 Art. 13).
- **It cannot adopt anything.** Green specimens license a phrase; adoption remains an owner
  act (Charter-P1 Art. 11).

And it cannot make the language larger by proving it safe. Any language stays safe by
saying almost nothing; any language becomes expressive by quietly reopening every forbidden
door. DG0 is the attempt to have the first without the second, and its only real instrument
is the corpus of things that still cannot be said.
