## Article 0 — What this charter is, and what it is not

**0.1 Purpose.** This charter fixes, in advance, the claim **lattice** for Lisp+
languagehood and succession: what each node means, what evidence discharges it, what
sentence becomes licensed when it is discharged, and — the point of the structure — which
edges exist between nodes and which do not. Its function is negative before it is
positive: it exists to make **evidence migrating from one node to another without
authorization** a detectable act rather than a rhetorical drift.

**0.1a Lattice, not ladder (Ruling 8).** The original charter presented five stations in
a numbered ladder and then had to spend Article 2 denying that the ladder meant anything.
That is a structure fighting its own shape. This revision replaces it with an explicit
graph: **nodes carry claims; edges carry declared dependencies; and a dependency is not
an implication.** No node's discharge licenses any other node's sentence. The only
construct in this charter that combines nodes is the **composite** (§1.COMP), which is a
conjunction requiring *both* conjuncts and is never earned by either alone.

**0.2 Prospective only.** This charter governs **succession claims made after its
adoption**. It does not rewrite, re-grade, or retroactively re-license any previously
adopted finding. Where a previously adopted record used different vocabulary, that record
stands as sealed; this charter binds the *next* claim, not the old one. *(Carried
unchanged.)*

**0.3 Its own standing.** This document is a candidate, and a revision of a candidate.
Nothing in it is adopted by being written, by being reviewed, by being agreed with, by
being directed by a ruling, or by any test turning green. Article 11 governs. **A ruling
that authorizes a revision is not an adoption of the revised text** (OWNER-RULINGS-1,
"This designation ruling does not adopt the campaign documents").

**0.4 Not a schedule.** Naming a node is not a plan to reach it, an estimate that it is
reachable, or a promise that reaching it is desirable. A node may stand unearned forever
without embarrassing the project. *(Carried unchanged.)*

**0.5 Where the naming law lives.** Article 15 (designation, per Ruling 1) and Article 16
(the identifier/keyword/case law recorded as owner-directed **candidate law awaiting
Parcel B**, per Ruling 6) are appended rather than inserted, so that every article number
cited by the frozen parcel documents still resolves to the same subject.

### L0 — Specification-owned languagehood (STRATIFIED, Ruling 8)

**1.L0.1 What the node asserts.** Lisp+ forms have normative meaning governed by the
Lisp+ specification and its judge, even though Common Lisp executes the implementation.
The five properties, stated separately because each is separately witnessed:

1. **Specification-owned syntax and validation** — a class of well-formed programs and a
   method of rejecting malformed ones, terminating before consequential activity.
2. **Operational meaning not conferred by host evaluation** — a Many Acts /0 form is
   *data* to Common Lisp; CL's evaluation rules do not determine what it means.
3. **Refusal behavior as a first-class semantic result**, not an error convention.
4. **Observable adjudicative consequences** — distinctions between executions that the
   specification, not the host, defines.
5. **Semantic jurisdiction distinct from CL evaluation** — the substrate executes the
   judge; it is not itself the law.

**1.L0.2 The stratification (Ruling 8: "L0 must distinguish what is earned on adopted One
Act /0 and Surface Account /0 evidence from what is additionally witnessed only by the
Many Acts candidate").**

**L0-a — adopted-evidence stratum. Status: EARNED at adopted standing.**
Witnessed by **One Act /0** (`language-act-0/ADOPTION-RECORD-2026-08-08.md`) and
**Surface Account /0**, both adopted. Properties 1, 3, 4 and 5 are witnessed at this
stratum at the adopted seam's scale — a specification-owned form class with validation
terminating before consequential activity, typed refusal as a result rather than an error
convention, adjudicative distinctions defined by the specification, and a judge that is
executed by but not authored by CL evaluation.

**L0-c — candidate-witnessed stratum. Status: WITNESSED AT CANDIDATE STANDING ONLY.**
Property 2 at *program* scale (program-as-data evaluation over a multi-act program),
property 3 at *program* scale (refusal as a semantic result of an act sequence rather than
of a single act), and the untaken arm as an inspectable object, are witnessed **only by
Many Acts /0 and its R1 repair, which are candidates awaiting owner disposition**
(OWNER-RULINGS-1, Ruling 2: "Many Acts /0 remains candidate").

*Drafting note, registered rather than smoothed:* the assignment of individual properties
to strata is the **reviser's judgment**, not owner-directed — the rulings direct the
stratification but do not partition the five properties. See **T8**.

**1.L0.3 The licensed sentences — one per stratum, never merged.**

At **L0-a** (adopted standing):

> Lisp+ programs possess specification-owned syntax and semantics: their normative
> meaning is defined by the Lisp+ specification and adjudicated by its judge, and is not
> inherited from Common Lisp evaluation. *(Witnessed on adopted One Act /0 and Surface
> Account /0 evidence.)*

At **L0-c** (candidate standing; the qualifier is part of the sentence and travels):

> The program-scale witnesses of specification-owned meaning — program-as-data
> evaluation, refusal as a program-level semantic result, and the untaken arm as an
> inspectable object — rest on **candidate** material (Many Acts /0 and its R1 repair)
> and are stated at candidate standing.

The chair's compression — *Lisp+ is a small experimental language* — is licensed **at
L0-a**, and any citation of it that leans on program-scale witnesses must carry the word
*candidate*.

**1.L0.4 Forbidden sentences.** "Common Lisp cannot express Lisp+ semantics" (the
category error corrected by Sol — CL implements the evaluator, so expressibility is not
and never was the criterion; **jurisdiction** is). "Lisp+ is a new Lisp." "Lisp+ is a
successor to Lisp" (Article 13). Any sentence that converts finiteness into a virtue
claim: L0 is compatible with a language of severely bounded expressivity, and says
nothing about generality. **And, new at P1:** any sentence that states an L0-c witness
without the word *candidate*, and any sentence that merges the two strata into one
undifferentiated "L0 is earned".

**1.L0.5 The contraction rule (carried from the original 1.L0.4, now an explicit edge).**
If the owner declines the Many Acts /0 candidate (or its R1 repair) such that the
program-scale witnesses lose standing, **L0-c lapses**, and every L0 claim in every
downstream document is restated at L0-a's size. Contraction is not a defeat and is not
reported as one; it is the edge doing its job.

**Status: NOT EARNED at its own name, and nothing beneath it sums toward it.**

**1.CI.1 What the node asserts when earned.** A mind **outside the construction and

**1.CI.2 Discharge condition.** An author who (a) did not build, review, adjudicate,
repair, or advise the implementation; (b) had no access to implementation source, repair
witnesses, execution transcripts, or holdout programs; (c) worked from published materials
only; and (d) whose exposure is disclosed and auditable — authors a program that is
accepted and behaves as they predicted, without evaluator modification. **One such author
is an existence proof, not a general result, and must be reported as one.**

**1.CI.3 The licensed sentence, when earned.**

> A clean-room outsider author, outside the construction and adjudication orbit and
> working from published materials only, authored a program that was accepted through the

**1.CI.4 Forbidden sentences.** "Authored against the public author guide" without an
exposure qualifier · "the first program by a mind outside the construction loop" applied

"stranger" applied to any P5 artifact or actor · **any sentence in which R-P4 and R-P5 are

**1.CI.5 What CI0 does not establish, even when earned.** That outsiders *in general* can
inhabit the language; that the language is useful; that it can be grown (that is DG0);

### DG0 — Disease-conserving generativity /0 *(replaces OG0; AUTHOR-AGNOSTIC, Ruling 8)*

**Status: NOT EARNED.**

**1.DG.0 What the rename and the author-agnostic rewrite did.** The original node "OG0 —
Disease-conserving generativity" required **outsider** authors inside its own definition,
which imported an inhabitation requirement into a generativity question and forced the
original charter to register tension **T4**. Ruling 8 directs that "disease-conserving
generativity should be author-agnostic" and that "outsider authorship and generativity may
later be conjoined, but neither should be smuggled into the definition of the other." The
outsider-authorship requirement is therefore **removed from DG0's definition and relocated
to the composite** (§1.COMP). **T4 is thereby RESOLVED-BY-LATTICE+COMPOSITE** (Article
14). The protocol body is `portable-judge-0/DG0-SKETCH-CANDIDATE-P1.md`.

**1.DG.1 What the node asserts when earned.** Authors — **of any provenance** — construct
**materially differently topologized, previously unseen applications** through a
**declared public extension mechanism**, without evaluator modification, and **without
turning a single inherited disease witness green**.

**1.DG.2 The licensed phrase, exactly (Ruling 8):**

> evidence of disease-conserving generativity across the tested unseen application
> topologies

The topologies are **named and counted** in every citation. The word "tested" is part of
the phrase and is not dropped.

**1.DG.3 The bound that makes it honest.** **Finite experiments cannot prove literal
open-endedness.** No number of new applications establishes that the language can grow
indefinitely; each run supplies evidence *across the topologies actually tested* and
nothing beyond them. The words **"open-ended", "unbounded", "arbitrary domains", and
"general-purpose"** are forbidden as descriptions of any DG0 result — and remain forbidden
"without the composite qualification" even where the composite is in play (§1.COMP.4).

**1.DG.4 The conserved quantity.** Article 8 is DG0's mechanical body: an extension is
lawful only if every prior disease still turns its witness **red, unmodified**. A door
reopened is a disease whose witness went quiet. Generativity purchased by subtracting an
impossibility is not generativity; it is a smaller language wearing a larger vocabulary.

**1.DG.5 Authorship is recorded, never a qualification.** A DG0 specimen's author may be a
construction-loop participant, an advisor, an outsider, or anyone else. Exposure is
**disclosed and filed before authoring** (Article 4.4) and is recorded as a qualifier on
the record — but non-outsider authorship **does not disqualify a DG0 specimen**. What it
does is precise and limited: such a specimen supplies **no CI0 evidence** and **cannot
contribute its author to the composite** (§1.COMP).

**1.DG.6 Declared dependency, and its unsettled part.** The DG0 protocol as sketched
requires each specimen's observations to match under J1 and a second judge J2 (sketch §2,
R6). **Which portable node supplies that J2 — and whether an oracle-mediated
language-layer J2 suffices for a specimen that exercises a declared extension
mechanism — is not settled by the rulings.** Registered as **T9**; not resolved here.

**1.DG.7 What DG0 does not establish, even when green.** Not outsider capability (that is
CI0). Not model benefit (that is LM0). Not open-endedness (§1.DG.3). Not full-stack or
language-layer conformance. Not usability. Not succession (Article 13).

### 1.COMP — COMPOSITE: Open-ended inhabitation *(Ruling 8)*

**Status: NOT EARNED. Not earnable by either conjunct alone.**

**1.COMP.1 Definition, per Ruling 8.**

> "Open-ended inhabitation" is a composite claim requiring both clean-room outsider
> authorship and disease-conserving generativity across materially different topologies.

Formally: **COMP-OEI = CI0 ∧ DG0**, where the DG0 conjunct is discharged **across
materially different topologies** and the CI0 conjunct is discharged **by the authors of
those specimens**. The composite is the *only* place in this charter where outsider
authorship and generativity are joined, and it joins them **without either being inside
the other's definition**.

**1.COMP.2 The conjunction is strict.** CI0 alone earns nothing of the composite. DG0
alone earns nothing of the composite. A DG0 campaign with participant authors plus a
separate CI0 existence proof by a different author **does not** compose: the conjunction
requires that the outsider authorship and the disease-conserving growth be *the same
work*, not two results filed adjacently. Anything short of that is two records, reported
as two.

**1.COMP.3 The licensed sentence, when earned.**

> Clean-room outsider authors, working from published materials only, grew previously
> unseen applications across N materially different tested topologies through the declared
> public extension mechanism, without evaluator modification, and without any inherited
> disease witness going quiet. The topologies are [named]; this is evidence across the
> topologies tested.

**1.COMP.4 Even the composite does not license the word "open-ended" as a description of
a result.** The composite is the *name of the claim*; its licensed sentence remains bounded
to the topologies actually tested and the authors actually recruited. "Open-ended
inhabitation" names what the project is asking about, never what a finite campaign
returned. *(Registered as **T10**: a claim named "open-ended" whose every licensed
utterance forbids the word is a standing invitation to drift. Named, not resolved.)*

## Article 2 — Non-substitutability, and what the edges do not carry

**2.1 The nodes are non-substitutable.** Each answers a different question; none is a
weaker or stronger version of another; **evidence discharging one never partially
discharges another.**

Named consequences, each a real drift this charter exists to block *(the original five,
carried, plus the ones the lattice adds)*:

- **Portable conformance does not earn DG0.** A second implementation agreeing on the
  vectors says nothing about whether new applications can be grown.
- **DG0 does not establish model benefit.** Authors growing new lawful domains says
  nothing about whether models author them *better* than they would author JSON.
- **P5 does not become independent implementation** because the authored program
  originated on another substrate. Authoring a program is not implementing a judge, and a
  different model substrate is not an independent orbit.
- **L0 does not imply anything above it.** Specification-owned meaning is compatible with
  a language nobody outside the project can use, that no second implementation realizes,
  and that no model benefits from.
- **R-P4 and R-P5 do not sum.** Same-author post-freeze expressibility + non-implementation
  authorship with disclosed exposure ≠ clean-room outsider inhabitation, at any weighting.
- **PortJ-L/0 does not partially discharge PortJ-F/0.** The layers were separated precisely
  because one is achievable now and the other is not; a green L/0 leaves F/0 exactly where
  it stood (§1.PF.3).
- **DG0 does not partially discharge CI0, and CI0 does not partially discharge DG0** —
  which is why the composite exists and why neither requirement sits inside the other's
  definition (§1.COMP.2).
- **L0-a does not license L0-c's sentences**, and L0-c's candidate witnesses do not
  upgrade L0-a's standing.

**2.2 Order is not implication; dependency is not implication.** The lattice's
presentation order is expository. A node may be attempted out of order; a node may be
discharged while a node it is drawn beneath stands open. Nothing may be inferred about
node X from the state of node Y except where an edge is declared explicitly in §1.0 — and
even a declared edge means only *"X cannot be attempted or claimed until Y"*, never *"Y
makes X true."*

**2.3 Partial discharge is a finding, not a fraction.** A node is earned or it is not.
Evidence short of the discharge condition is recorded as a **named evidence record with
its own licensed sentence** (as §1.E does), never as a percentage, never as "nearly",
never as "on track".

**2.4 The evidence register is a terminus, not a staging area.** A record filed in §1.E
does not "await promotion". If later work discharges a node, that node is discharged by
*its own* evidence; the register's records are not retroactively re-read as partial
instalments of it.

## Article 4 — Implementer independence

*Carried from the frozen original; designation swept, node names updated.*

**4.1 Independence is partly a PROVENANCE claim, not only a code claim.** Whether an
implementation is independent is settled by *who wrote it and what they had seen*, not by
inspecting the artifact.

**4.2 The disqualifying relations.** An implementation is **not** independent if its
author (a) participated in the construction loop of the canonical implementation; (b)
participated in adjudication, review, repair, or advisory analysis of it; (c) had access
to canonical implementation source, repair witnesses, execution transcripts, or holdout
programs; or (d) imported, ported, transliterated, or consulted canonical implementation
code.

**4.3 The named consequence.** **A second evaluator written in another language by a
construction-loop participant is a PORT, not a clean-room witness.** Language difference
is not independence. Substrate difference is not independence. A rewrite by the same mind
is one mind's reading twice.

**4.4 Disclosure is mandatory and is data, not disqualification-by-itself.** Every
implementer files an exposure statement before the run. An undisclosed exposure discovered
afterward **voids the conformance claim** for that campaign; a disclosed exposure is
recorded in the licensed sentence as a qualifier. *(Under §1.DG.5 this rule also governs
DG0 specimen authors, for whom disclosure is recorded but non-outsider status is not
disqualifying.)*

**4.5 Standing example, filed so it cannot be forgotten.** Sol authored the adopted
Process Journal /0 packet and supplied the R1 generation-seam analysis. Sol is therefore
disqualified as an independent implementer for **PortJ-L/0 and PortJ-F/0**, and as a
clean-room outsider for **CI0** — permanently, and regardless of how the P5 result reads.
This is not a demerit; it is the price of having been useful.

**4.6 Independence is spent, never restored.** Once a mind has seen the canonical
internals or advised the construction, no subsequent forgetting, session reset, context
clearing, or substrate swap restores its eligibility. Statelessness cannot be verified
from outside; the provenance record can.

## Article 5 — Public versus hidden evidence

*Carried unchanged from the frozen original.*

**5.1** The vector set has two halves. The **public** half is published with the

**5.3** Any hidden vector that leaks — by publication, by inclusion in a packet, by

sheet. *(P1 compliance note: this revision authors no hidden vectors and contains none.)*

## Article 6 — First-run versus repaired conformance

*Carried unchanged from the frozen original.*

**6.1** First-attempt results and post-repair results are **always reported separately**,

**6.2** A first-run transcript is preserved **unaltered**, with its exit code captured

**6.4** Repairs to the *implementation under test* are permitted within a campaign if the

## Article 7 — Specification ambiguities and errata

*Carried unchanged from the frozen original.*

**7.1 Underdetermination is a specification defect, not an implementer error.** When two
implementations diverge and the specification text does not settle which is correct, the
divergence is **underdetermined**.

**7.2 The mandatory response.** An underdetermination yields, in this order: (a) an
**erratum** to the specification, published; (b) a **new frozen seed** incorporating it;
(c) a **new campaign** run against that seed.

**7.3 The prohibition that gives 7.2 its teeth.** The second implementation is **never
tutored into imitation.** It is not told what the canonical implementation does; it is not
shown the canonical behavior and asked to match; the erratum states what the *law* is, not
what the incumbent *did*. An implementation corrected by being shown its target has
demonstrated obedience, not conformance, and its subsequent agreement carries no
evidential weight.

**7.4 The old campaign does not promote.** A campaign that surfaced an underdetermination
ends where it ended. Its outcome is reported with the underdetermination named. It is not
retroactively graded green by the erratum that followed it.

**7.5 Errata are cumulative and public.** The erratum record is part of the specification's
governed surface and is cited by every subsequent conformance claim.

## Article 8 — Disease-corpus conservation

*Carried unchanged from the frozen original; it is DG0's mechanical body.*

**8.1 The disease corpus is the conserved quantity.** Every impossibility the project
claims exists twice: as law-prose, and as a planted executable disease whose witness must
turn **red**.

**8.2 The conservation law.**

> An extension is lawful only if every prior disease still turns its witness red,
> unmodified. New vocabulary and composition may be added; no old impossibility may be
> re-said. Growth is measured in what still cannot be uttered.

**8.3 Inheritance is total and unamendable.** Each generation inherits the full ancestral
disease corpus as a floor. Witnesses are not rewritten, retuned, relaxed, retired,
re-scoped, or marked expected-to-change. A witness whose red depends on a detail that an
extension legitimately alters is **re-expressed to preserve the same impossibility**, and
the re-expression is itself reviewed and recorded — it is never simply dropped.

**8.4 The detection rule.** A door reopened is a disease whose witness went quiet. Silence
is the alarm: a disease that no longer fires is treated as a **reopened impossibility**
until proven to be a harness fault, never the other way around.

**8.5 Teeth-check the conservation itself.** A conservation suite that has never been seen
to fail is untested, not passing. Each campaign plants at least one deliberate violation
and exhibits the corpus catching it before any clean run is credited.

## Article 10 — Claim language after green, red, or ambiguous outcomes

*Carried unchanged from the frozen original.*

**10.1 After GREEN.** Only the node's licensed sentence, verbatim, with its qualifiers
attached. The qualifiers travel with the claim into every downstream document — abstracts,
front doors, commit messages, and summaries included. A licensed sentence stripped of its
qualifiers is an unlicensed claim.

**10.2 After RED.** The red is **preserved, not repaired inside the campaign**, and it is
publishable. The licensed form names what failed and at what scope. A red result is never
described as "inconclusive", never quietly re-run until green, and never re-scoped after
the fact so that the failing case falls outside the claim.

**10.3 After AMBIGUOUS.** An outcome that neither discharges nor refutes is reported as
**not detected**, with the reason. Specifically: **a straddle-zero result is "not
detected", never "null confirmed"** (house rider). A null may be *confirmed* only under a
pre-declared equivalence margin δ, with the entire confidence interval inside [−δ, +δ],
and δ anchored to the question's effect scale rather than to the instrument's noise floor.

**10.4 Bounds are about magnitude, not existence.** Tightening an exclusion bound is a
statement about **magnitude-if-it-exists**, never evidence against existence. "We narrowed
the possible size of the effect" is licensed; "we weakened the case for the effect" is not.

**10.5 Counts.** Counts are stated as **"X executed + Y N/A"**, never rolled up into a
single total (house rider). "68 executed + 3 N/A" is the licensed form; "71 passed" is not.

**10.6 Hedging is not demotion.** Wrapping an over-large claim in "possibly", "arguably",
or "may suggest" while it keeps its position and its role demotes nothing. Demote by
**recategorizing** — a different node, a different section, an explicit smaller size —
never by softening adjectives.

**10.7 Verification is shown or declared compressed.** Any sentence containing "verified",
"traced", "checked", or "confirmed" either exhibits its load-bearing step or is written as
"traced, compressed", so the compression is visible and challengeable.

**10.8 A diagnosis is a claim.** An explanation of *why* something failed is subject to the
same evidentiary standard as any other claim. Either exhibit the evidence or write "cause
unverified". A wrong diagnosis closes an investigation; a missing one leaves it open.
