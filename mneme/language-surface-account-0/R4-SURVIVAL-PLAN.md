# Surface Account /0 — R4 Survival Plan

**Author:** Claude Fable 5 (JURIST seat). Same-family cross-checks, not an
independent audit. **Date:** 2026-08-04. **R1-corrected** per the owner
adjudication Section G (five ruled points; see `R1-AMENDMENT-LOG.md`).

**The assessed risk this plan answers:** *candidate accretion without
adoption or retirement; the empty stranger seat becomes ceremonial.* The
cure is one governed candidate round that puts the lane **inside the
release organism** with real gates, followed by an explicit decision path
that ends in adoption, retirement, or a named halt — never in an
accumulating shelf.

**This opening round is different by design:** it contains only the docket,
contract, and non-production probe, returned before merge. Everything below
is the plan for the **future** implementation round; none of it is begun
here. A directory-only production candidate is forbidden and this plan
leaves it no room.

## 7. Closure / adoption / retirement decision path

At the end of the candidate round, the return goes to the owner with
exactly three exits, each named in the return itself:

1. **Adopt** — the lane enters governed standing; floors become binding;
   the stranger-audit debt is docketed (this family has audited itself
   throughout; the empty stranger seat is filled or explicitly waived by
   the owner, never silently). **The canonical cross-surface front-door
   ruling is part of the Adopt exit** (R2 adjudication, Section F,
   sharpening R1 Section G's "include or be followed by"; coexistence law
   clause 5): without it the composite risks becoming another ceremonial
   entry point standing beside the native doors — the exact accretion
   failure this plan exists to forbid — and with it, the direction of new
   callers to the composite stops being convention and becomes adopted
   law. And in the ruling's own words: **Surface /3 may not open merely
   because an implementation exists and passes; it remains blocked until
   the owner explicitly names the canonical authority or explicitly
   accepts labelled dual authority**;
2. **Retire the candidate** — the lane is closed as
   not-adopted-with-reasons, bytes retained, floors removed, and the
   docket's alternatives (including `GOVERNING PROMISE REQUIRES REVISION`)
   return to the owner's table;
3. **Halt named-open** — only with a dated reason and a named reopening
   condition; an undated, unreasoned halt is the ceremonial accretion this
   plan exists to forbid.

No fourth exit exists. "Still on the shelf" is not a standing.
