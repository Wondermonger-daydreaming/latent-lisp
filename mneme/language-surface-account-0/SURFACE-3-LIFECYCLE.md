# Surface Account /0 — The Surface /3 Lifecycle Under the Recommended Lineage

**Author:** Claude Fable 5 (JURIST seat). Same-family cross-checks, not an
independent audit. **Date:** 2026-08-04.

**This is a design document, not an implementation path.** It walks the
commission's seven literal lifecycle steps under the recommended lineage
(`NATIVE-COMPOSITE SUCCESSOR LAW RECOMMENDED`, `ARCHITECTURE-DOCKET.md`
§6). The charge is explicit: if any step cannot be made true, the verdict
must reflect it. Each step below states the mechanism it rests on, where
that mechanism lives, and — **amended after the fresh-context review
(F6)** — its **mechanism class**, because the classes are not the same and
flattening them overstated two steps:

| Class | Meaning | Steps |
|---|---|---|
| **STRUCTURAL** | made true by the design itself — code shape, closed tables, fail-closed checks | 1, 5, 7 |
| **PROCEDURAL** | owner-owned acts the design fully prepares and cannot pre-empt | 2, 4 |
| **GOVERNANCE-DEPENDENT** | the design **enables** the step and removes every mechanical alternative, but its truth requires future commission text no design can compel | 3 (positive half), 6 |

None of this opens Surface /3, and nothing here is implemented.

---

## Step 1 — "Surface Account /0 closes over exactly seven heads."

**Mechanism class: STRUCTURAL. Made true by:** the closed manifest law
(`SURFACE-ACCOUNT-0-CONTRACT-CANDIDATE.md` I.1). The manifest is a
compile-time constant of exactly the seven measured head keys
(`SEVEN-HEAD-MANIFEST-CANDIDATE.tsv`, uniqueness asserted in code this
round: `total=7 unique=7 ok=YES`, `CARTOGRAPHY-NOTES.md` §11), resolved by
`EQ` against stored symbols, with the collision alarm exercised (Control 3).
There is no eighth row and no way to add one at runtime. Closure of the
*lane* itself (adoption, freeze) remains an owner decision in the future
governed round — "closes over exactly seven heads" is a statement about the
manifest's extension, not a claim that this round closed anything.

## Step 2 — "An owner later opens Surface /3's language-contract phase."

**Mechanism class: PROCEDURAL. Made true by:** doing nothing now. This step
is owner-owned; the /0 design's only obligation is to leave it genuinely open —
no Surface /3 path, package, system, form, contract, or convenience API
exists in this lane (the parcel proves the absences), and the preserved-next-
boundary condition (an accepted, *integrated* implementation of this
lifecycle, then an explicit owner commission) is restated in
`R4-SURVIVAL-PLAN.md` §8 as the gate the production round must satisfy
before this step can ever fire.

## Step 3 — "Surface /3 defines language forms and semantics but owns no request, occurrence, receipt, term-codec, or expansion-account engine."

**Mechanism class: negative half STRUCTURAL; positive half
GOVERNANCE-DEPENDENT — the design enables this step but cannot itself make
it true (F6).** Two mechanisms:

- *Negative (nothing to attach to):* the composite exposes no registry,
  callback, generic-function seam, or package scan (I.1; step 7 below). A
  Surface /3 lane cannot register its heads into the account machinery even
  by accident; the only way in is **a governed Account successor
  introducing its own distinct manifest** under the versioned coexistence
  law (R3.1-C: /0 declarations never move; no "manifest movement" exists),
  which is Account-owned and owner-governed.
- *Positive (nothing it would need to build):* the standing composite front
  door plus the returned /1 successor contract
  (`SURFACE-ACCOUNT-0-CONTRACT-CANDIDATE.md` Part II) means an S3 commission
  arrives to find the account service **already existing and already
  governed**, with a specified extension path for exactly its situation.
  Cloning pressure is removed by supply — that much the design does. But
  cloning **permission** is removed only by a prohibition in the S3
  commission text, **a document that does not exist and that no design can
  compel** — this half of the step is a promise about future governance,
  and it is labelled as such rather than claimed as made-true.

The head-ownership precedent is already in the tree: Surface /0 owns five
macros it does not observe; Surface /1 observes them without owning them
(the measured owner/observer asymmetry, `CARTOGRAPHY-NOTES.md` §11).
Surface /3 slots into exactly that pattern: owner of its forms, owner of no
engine.

## Step 4 — "A separately commissioned Surface Account /1 successor adds the exact new heads and module bindings, moving manifest/grammar/procedure identities and versions as required."

**Mechanism class: PROCEDURAL (owner-commissioned, design-prepared). Made
true by:** the returned conditional contract (Part II): the /1
manifest carries the seven inherited rows plus the exact new heads, three
provenance roles separate; the Account-owned grammar is already adjudicated
(pinned S1 codec eliminated on measured evidence —
`TERM-GRAMMAR-DECISION.md` §2 — so /1 does not re-litigate it); /1's
manifest, grammar, procedure, and policy identities/versions are **its own
distinct declarations** in the `lisp-plus-surface-account` domain,
introduced under the /1 commission — the step-text word "moving" is
satisfied by this introduction of distinct declarations under the
coexistence law, never by mutating /0's, **which never move** (R3.1-C).
The /1 opening ruling must also adjudicate
supersession vs labelled dual authority for the seven inherited heads —
docketed, not decided, in `ARCHITECTURE-DOCKET.md` §2-D.

## Step 5 — "Old Account /0 requests fail closed under incompatible binding movement unless an explicit compatibility law is separately accepted."

**Mechanism class: STRUCTURAL. Made true by the versioned coexistence law**
(contract II.1b — adjudication Section C, adopted verbatim; this paragraph
was rewritten in R1: the R0 mechanism claimed /1 manifest movement would be
caught by /0's Door 2 comparing against "the live manifest," which wrongly
implied the /0 declaration could move — **the /0 manifest is immutable, and
no /1 movement reaches into /0**). The actual mechanism:

- **The fail-closed boundary is /1's door:** /0 requests are a distinct
  sealed species carrying the /0 manifest identity/version; a /0 request
  presented to a /1 door fails **before invocation** as
  `incompatible-account-version` or `wrong-request-species` — a typed
  protocol refusal of that door.
- **/0 requests remain /0 requests**, accepted only by the /0 Door 2, and a
  /0 request presented to the unchanged /0 door may remain valid under /0's
  closed law — /1's existence invalidates nothing there.
- **No compatibility is implied, offered, or default**; a compatibility law
  would be its own separately accepted artifact (clause "unless … separately
  accepted", carried verbatim).
- Inside /0, Door 2's binding recheck detects only corruption/tampering of
  the immutable manifest (an integrity alarm) — it is defence-in-depth, not
  the version mechanism.

This is why the /0 manifest identity/version must exist at all (I.1): the
species/version separation at /1's boundary needs an exact /0 identity to
refuse against.

## Step 6 — "Surface /3's inhabited gates use the selected Account successor."

**Mechanism class: GOVERNANCE-DEPENDENT — enabled by the design, made true
only by future commission text (F6).** What the design supplies: the S3
commission would inherit a standing, integrated account front door (the
production round's inhabited two-language specimen, `R4-SURVIVAL-PLAN.md`
§5, demonstrates exactly the usage pattern S3's gates would follow), and no
mechanical alternative exists — no native door can serve new heads (both
native manifests are closed enumerations, measured) and no S3-local machine
can lawfully exist (step 3). What the design cannot supply: the requirement
itself, which must be **carried by the S3 lifecycle text** — a future
document. This step is a promise the design prepares completely and cannot
sign.

## Step 7 — "No runtime registration point ever appears."

**Mechanism class: STRUCTURAL. Made true by construction, at every layer:**
the manifest is an `ECASE`-equivalent closed table (I.1); admission to the
/0 inspector is an **immutable** closed tagged union over exact public
predicates — never extended by anything; a future /1 ships its **own
distinct inspector** at its own public address (contract I.8, II.2/II.5b;
R2 Section B); the refusal keying is category-first with a closed positive
phase enumeration
(`REFUSAL-AND-CONDITION-JURISDICTION.md` §4, as amended); the /1
mint contract carries the same prohibition permanently (Part II.1). There
is no `defgeneric` seam, no MOP dispatch, no plugin hook, no package
discovery, no arbitrary macro walker — and the production round's gates must
include a planted-fault control that attempts a runtime extension and shows
it refused (the Control discipline: a gate that has never fired is untested,
not passing).

## The honest residue

**Five of the seven steps are made true by the design or by
already-owner-owned procedure (1, 5, 7 structural; 2, 4 procedural). Two —
step 3's positive half and step 6 — are governance-dependent: enabled and
left without mechanical alternative, but true only if future commission
text says so.** *(This paragraph replaced "Every step is made true as
design" after review F6 — that sentence was one notch above the evidence
for exactly those two steps.)* Three further things this document does not
and cannot claim:

1. It does not claim the lifecycle is *implemented* — that is the production
   round's charge, and the preserved-next-boundary requires the accepted,
   integrated implementation before Surface /3 may begin.
2. It does not claim the /1 commission will open, or bind the owner's hand
   on supersession vs labelled dual authority — that decision is docketed
   with its options and consequences stated.
3. It does not claim the strict-count reading of the governing promise is
   satisfied — under that reading no candidate satisfies it
   (`ARCHITECTURE-DOCKET.md` §4); these seven steps are the ownership
   reading, which the commission's own lifecycle text adopts.

The charge — every step shown achievable — is met under that reading **at
the mechanism classes stated above**: five steps made true by design or
procedure, two governance-dependent steps enabled to the limit of what any
design can do, with their dependence on future commission text named rather
than flattened. The verdict in `ARCHITECTURE-DOCKET.md` §6 carries the same
sizing. If the owner reads the commission's "make this lifecycle literal"
as requiring all seven steps to be design-compelled, then steps 3 and 6 are
not compellable by any candidate — a mint (Candidate C) has exactly the
same dependence on future S3-commission text for what S3's gates *use* —
and the owner should weigh that as an inherent limit of the charge, not a
defect any lineage escapes.
