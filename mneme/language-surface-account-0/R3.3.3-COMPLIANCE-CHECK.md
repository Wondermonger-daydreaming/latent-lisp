## 2. The total walk, read line by line

`SA0-SCAN-RESERVED` (`probes/probe-identity.lisp:629-751`). The examination
below is the contested step exhibited, not a claim that it was checked.

**Every non-`NIL` terminal tail is rejected, at both parities.** The walk stands
at a KEY position with `REST = (CDR TAIL)` at the VALUE position, and it
distinguishes the two faces the R3.3.2 walk conflated:

* case (1), `:711`, `(not (consp tail))` — the atom reached **after a complete
  pair** (`(:A :B . :TAIL)`). This is the face the returned walk's `while`
  swallowed. Message names `~D complete pair(s)` and the atom;
* case (3), `:738`, `(not (consp rest))` — the atom reached **inside** a pair
  (`(:A . :B)`, or `(:C . :TAIL)` at an odd offset). The R3.3.2 walk *did* reject
  this face, but under case (2)'s wording ("ends in the indicator with no
  value"), which was untrue of it; case (3) now carries its own accurate text.

Both are exercised in fact: arm 1 refuses "after 1 complete pair(s)", arm 2
"after 2 complete pair(s)" — the lawful cell shifts the parity by one pair and
the same case still fires.

**The five-way enumeration is complete and correct.** Written at `:652-680` and
implemented at `:707-750`: (0) `NIL` — the one lawful exit; (1) non-`NIL` atomic
tail; (2) cons with `NIL` rest — odd list; (3) cons with non-cons rest — dotted
value position; (4) already-visited cons — cycle; else a well-formed pair. The
source's own closure argument is the right one and it is sound: *at every step
`TAIL` is `NIL`, a non-cons atom, or a cons; in the last case `REST` is `NIL`, a
non-cons atom, or a cons.* Three × (implicitly one) branches with no residue.
There is no sixth case.

**The cycle detection is genuinely total.** The table is `:test 'eq` (`:704`) —
object identity, so no structural comparison of a circular datum is ever
attempted. Order matters and is right: the `gethash` test at `:719` precedes the
`setf` at `:727`, so a cons is checked *before* it is recorded, and every cons
the walk stands on is recorded before the walk steps off it.

One deviation from the letter of my brief, resolved in the source's favour and
shown rather than asserted: **the walk records `TAIL` only, not `REST`** — one of
the two conses of the `CDDR` step. That is not a hole. The visited set is exactly
the set of *stood-on* conses, and the pigeonhole argument runs on stood-on
conses: if the walk did not terminate it would take infinitely many steps, each
either exiting or standing on a cons drawn from the finite set of conses
reachable from `PLIST`, so some cons is stood on twice and case (4) fires the
second time. Recording `REST` would add nothing — `REST` is not a step position,
and on any structure where it later *becomes* one (an odd-period ring, where key
and value positions swap each lap) it is recorded at that moment, before it is
stepped off. The docstring anticipates exactly this and names the case a
tortoise-and-hare would have to argue separately (`:691-696`). **The pigeonhole
argument is sound as written**, including for rho-shaped tails whose cycle
excludes the head, and it needs no premise about the input's shape.

**Nothing steps into an error.** `(cddr tail)` is evaluated only at the top of a
*subsequent* iteration, and cases (2) and (3) signal before any such iteration
can begin, so the walk never takes `CDR` of an atom.

**Rejection reads and never writes.** `sa0-scan-reserved` mutates nothing but its
own local hash table; there is no truncation at the malformation, no completion,
no re-ordering. This is measured, not trusted: `sa0-ct-capture` records the head
cons, every spine cons in order, each `CAR`, and the terminal object **by
identity**, and `sa0-ct-unchanged-p` re-compares them with a fixed number of `EQ`
tests (never a structural walk of a circular plant). All three arms print
`unchanged by identity YES`.

**Two notes carried as observations, neither a gap.**

1. The image-wide `gatherings 0` line is read through `sa0-epoch-gatherings`,
   which counts marks *on the current cell* — with the carrier restored to `NIL`
   it would read 0 whether or not a gather had ever happened. The claim is
   nevertheless sound, because it rides on `sa0-election-count` = 0, which lives
   on a second interned symbol that nothing in the source replaces, and no gather
   can occur without a won election. The source documents this asymmetry exactly

   is honest rather than lucky.
2. `sa0-cs-reserved-count` (`probe-init-schedule.lisp:1271-1280`), the R3.3.2
   *harness* measure, is still a `while (consp tail)` walk and would not
   terminate on a ring. It is disclosed as a measure and not a law, and it is
   never called on a carrier-total plant — that role measures with the total

## 4. The comparator-hazard finding — my judgment

**What happened.** The first disease-reintroduction comparator **hung**. Under
the restored `while (consp tail)` law the arms' *readers* — `sa0-election-count`,
`sa0-epoch-gatherings`, `sa0-identity-ready-p` — are called with a malformed
carrier still installed, and against the circular plant a reader of that carrier
never comes back. A harness calling them directly does not *report* the returned
law's worst failure; it **becomes** it, and a section that hangs delivers no
verdict at all.

**The fix.** `sa0-ct-bounded-answer` (`probe-init-schedule.lisp:1533-1550`) runs
each reader in its own thread under `*sa0-hard-bound-seconds*` via
`sa0-ct-bounded-call`, and maps the three outcomes to the reader's own answer,
`:REFUSED`, or **`:HUNG`** as a distinct value.

**Is the fix sound?** Yes, on three grounds I checked rather than assumed:

1. **No reader can hang the comparator.** Every reader goes through
   `sa0-ct-bounded-answer`; `sa0-ct-bounded-call` wraps the call in
   `sb-ext:with-timeout` *and* joins with `sa0-join`, which itself carries the
   hard bound and returns `:TIMEOUT` rather than waiting. Both the wait and the
   join are bounded, so the main line always proceeds. The same discipline
   already governs the loads (`sa0-cs-run-load`).
2. **A hang is a verdict, not an absence.** `:HUNG` is a *failing* value, not a
   silence: no check accepts it. `INIT-CT-DOTTED-REJECTED-AND-PLIST-UNCHANGED`
   requires `(eq :refused *ct-dotted-ready*)` and `(eq :refused
   *ct-dotted-gatherings*)`; the arm-2 and arm-3 checks require `:refused`
   likewise. `:HUNG`, `:READY` and `:NOT-READY` all fail. This matters: the
   printed sentence is the stronger one — *no reader can extract a readiness
   answer from a carrier this source will not read, and it finds that out in
   bounded time* — and the predicate actually tests that sentence.
3. **The `:HUNG` path is not untested decoration.** The disease run exhibits it
   firing: `disease-carrier-total.txt` line 81, `arm 3 readiness after HUNG`,
   with the comparator still completing and exiting 1. A gate that has never
   fired is untested; this one has been shown able to fire, by the very
   comparator it was built for.

**Was the first attempt honestly disclosed?** Yes, in two places and at two
depths. The committed source says it in its own voice at the point of the fix —
*"THE THIRD OUTCOME IS NOT DECORATION, and this harness learned it the hard
way"* — and the transcript prints the reason before any number ("A harness that
asked them directly would not report that failure — it would become it"). The
`R1-AMENDMENT-LOG.md` R3.3.3 entry names it as history rather than as design:
*"a disease-reintroduction comparator (5/7 FAIL, exit 1) whose own first run
HUNG … a hazard found and closed mid-round"*. That is disclosure, not silent
replacement. **I record it as the round's best moment of method:** the hazard was
in the *instrument*, it was found by running the instrument, and the repair
converted an absence of evidence into a nameable failing outcome.

**One limit I will not smooth.** The comparator run under the disease leaves two
loader threads spinning past the 60 s bound (arms 2 and 3 report `load join
TIMEOUT`); the process nonetheless terminated with exit 1, which I take from the
observed `CHILD-EXIT 1` rather than from any claim about SBCL's exit semantics.
That is the sick path only; the healthy path joins every thread `DONE`.
